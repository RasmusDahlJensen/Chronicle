# M6.4 — Raids and confederations

**Feature / status:** the fourth slice of M6, "War and peace" (VISION.md; the plan is in the [M6.1 brief](m6-1.md)). Status: planned.

**Why:** VISION.md's "Raids": "Before state-level government, conflicts are raids: short actions that steal food or stores, burn buildings and cause casualties, without territorial change." Its M6 "Grounded in history": "threat drives consolidation: a strong or raiding neighbour raises the pull of unions among those it threatens, and herders beside rich farmers gain from raiding and from confederating." M6.1–M6.3 gave civilizations armies, wars and their ravages; the tribes beside them have so far been only neighbours to absorb. This slice lets herders and hungry tribes raid rich farmers, makes walls and armies answer, and lets raiding tribes draw their kin into confederations.

**Observable outcome:**
- **Raids:** once a year a tribe's band next to a civilization's land weighs a raid: hunger and herding push it, a rich store pulls it, and its warriors' odds against the soldiers and walls there decide how likely it is. A raid takes food (exact), may burn a building, kills on both sides (war deaths) and moves no border. Each raid is an event with its causes.
- **Walls answer:** a raided region remembers it (fading over decades); the memory calls for walls there (the Build need for defence), and its realm feels the threat: it keeps more soldiers and looks harder for shelter in a union.
- **Confederations:** a tribe that has raided lately may draw a smaller tribe of its lineage beside it into one confederation (the smaller joins whole); an event.
- **The game:** raids and confederations in the chronicle (the War kind and the Peoples kind); the Region tab says when a region was raided; the ledger's world now counts raids.

## Acceptance

- **Tests:** a band weighs raids by hunger, herding, riches and odds (walls and soldiers lower them); a raid moves food exactly, kills on both sides as war deaths, may burn a building, moves no border; the raid memory calls for walls and raises the threat its realm feels; a confederation passes a whole tribe; the invariants hold.
- **Study (five seeds, 3,000 years):** raids a century and where (tribes beside farmers), walls built after raids, confederations; M6.2–M6.3's health and the M5 gates still hold.
- **The complete `npm run check` passes.**
