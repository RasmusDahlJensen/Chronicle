# U.5 — The chronicle

**Feature / status:** the fifth and last building slice of milestone U (the plan is in the [U.1 brief](u-1.md)). Status: verified, awaiting user review (VISION.md rule 2).

**Why:** VISION.md calls Chronicle "a story simulator: every system exists to produce causes and consequences an observer can follow". The game shows only the five latest notable events (the ticker), the latest 40 of each realm (U.2) and the stories (U.3). The whole chronicle, back to year 0, is reachable only in the lab's raw list. The chronicle also repeats itself: a drought year can bring a dozen famine lines, one per realm.

**Observable outcome:**
- **The chronicle view:** the top bar's third view (Map, Ledger, Chronicle). It reads the whole history, newest first, a page at a time, back to year 0.
  - **Filters:**
    - by kind:
      - peoples and realms;
      - settlements and building;
      - knowledge;
      - faith and culture;
      - trade and diplomacy;
      - exploration;
      - hardship (drought, famine, neglect, arrears);
    - by people (the events one realm or tribe acts in);
    - notable only, or everything.
  - **Each event:**
    - its year, its text in the peoples' names, and all its causes in words with their weights;
    - "Show on the map", which returns to the map at its settlement or region and selects it.
- **Curation:** a run of events of one type for one people, each within 25 years of the next, becomes one line with its count and years. A famine run gives its deaths in all ("Famine strikes the Drailese … And 5 more like it since year 1,471: 412,000 died in all"). The most important events (a wonder, a faith founded) keep their own lines. The ticker uses the same curation, so it no longer repeats one people's famine.

## Acceptance

- **Tests:**
  - **Paging:** the chronicle request pages backward from any event id with filters (kinds, people, least importance), exactly matching the history's events.
  - **Curation:** it folds only same-type, same-people runs within 25 years (the people an event is about, not its first actor), and never the most important events. Its counts and summed deaths match the events.
  - **Kinds:** every event type belongs to exactly one kind.
  - **The contract:** the request and reply are validated.
- **Browser:**
  - the chronicle view lists events newest first and reads older pages;
  - a kind filter and a people filter narrow it;
  - "Show on the map" returns to the map at that place;
  - the ticker shows a run once (no two of its lines belong to one run).
- **The complete `npm run check` passes.**

## Decisions and defaults

- **Pages from the host** (protocol 37): `GET /api/simulation/chronicle`, a new worker request. Its query:
  - `before` (an event id), `limit` (1–200);
  - `types` (a comma list of event types; unknown types are refused);
  - `polity` (events it acts in);
  - `min` (least importance).

  The page carries the matching events, newest first, with the names of their peoples, the history's run, epoch and event count, and `next`, where the next page reads from. A page reads at most 50,000 events, so a narrow filter over a long history can come back short with more to read; the view offers to read on. The reader is `chroniclePage` in `src/simulation/observer.ts`, tested against the history itself.
- **Kinds are the observer's** (`src/observer/chronicle.ts`), not the simulation's. Every event type maps to exactly one kind (the type system and a test enforce it):
  - peoples and realms;
  - settlements and building;
  - knowledge;
  - faith and culture;
  - trade and diplomacy;
  - exploration;
  - hardship;
  - war and raids (from M6);
  - development controls (off by default).

  The browser sends the chosen kinds' types.
- **Curation** (`curate`, shared by the chronicle and the ticker):
  - **Runs:** events of one type for one people, each within 25 years of the next, are one line ("And 7 more like it since year 1,475").
    - **The people** is the one the event is about: the realm taking in a band or joined by another, the tribe a group breaks away from; else its first actor.
    - **Famines:** a famine line sums its deaths ("1,203,000 died in all").
  - **Not folded:** events of importance 0.3 or more stand on their own lines (two wonders are two lines). Droughts and famines are the exception: their importance grows with their toll, and their runs are what curation is for.
  - **Other peoples:** famines of different peoples in one year stay separate lines; folding them under one name would misstate whose deaths they were.
  - **The ticker** curates its notable events the same way before taking the newest five.
- **The view:**
  - **Where it lives:** the top bar's third stage view (Map, Ledger, Chronicle), over the map like the ledger.
  - **Filters:**
    - kinds as checkboxes;
    - a people list (the civilizations, and the people selected on the map, tribe or realm);
    - "Notable events only" (importance 0.05, as the realm panel's history; on by default).
  - **Pages:** a new filter or history reads from the newest again; "Read older events" reads on, back to year 0.
    - **A page from another run or reset epoch** (read across a reset) starts the list afresh.
    - **When the history has moved on**, a button offers to read from the newest.
  - **Kept state:** the view stays mounted once opened (hidden on the map), so its filters and pages survive "Show on the map".
  - **Each line:**
    - its year or years and its text in the peoples' names;
    - every cause in words with its weight;
    - "Show on the map", which goes to its settlement (if standing) or its region, selects it and returns focus to the map.

## Review

The independent review (an agent) probed the curation on real histories and the route with injected requests. It measured a 50,000-event scan of a 1,000,000-event chronicle at under 1 ms. Its findings, all resolved:
1. **Curation named the wrong people (material):** the run key was the first actor. For bands taken in that is the band, so bands of one tribe taken by different realms folded under one realm (18 lines in a probe), and unions never folded. Fixed with each type's main people; the test's fixture now uses the simulation's actor order.
2. **Famine folding contradicted the brief (material):** one year's famines of several peoples were one line under one name, while one people's famine years were never folded. Fixed as above, with the brief's wording made consistent.
3. **Browser acceptance unchecked (material):** reading older pages, the place "Show on the map" goes to, the order and years of the lines, and the ticker's curation are now asserted.
4. **Smaller fixes:**
   - **Paging across a reset:** pages from another history start afresh.
   - **Errors:** no error or Retry is shown with every kind unchecked.
   - **The people filter:** the chosen people stays listed.
   - **Focus:** the read buttons keep focus while reading.
   - **Kept state:** filters and pages survive "Show on the map".
   - **Wording:** "The history has N newer events" (they are the history's, not the filter's).
   - **The route:**
     - its 400s carry no retry-after;
     - the type list's limit follows `EVENT_TYPES`;
     - the run id reuses the instance's schema;
     - the README says a request starts a world's simulation.
   - **Not folded:** important events are never folded.
   - **Tests:**
     - the route's older page has its five events;
     - a 120,000-event chronicle with sparse matches is read exactly across the scan limit.
