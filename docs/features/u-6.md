# U.6 — The remaining observer views

**Feature / status:** a sixth slice of milestone U, added before the close-out (the plan is in the [U.1 brief](u-1.md)). Status: verified, awaiting user review (VISION.md rule 2).

**Why:** VISION.md's U acceptance asks that "everything M1–M5 simulates that VISION.md's 'Observer views' names has a place in the UI". Checked against "Observer views" after U.5, four things M1–M5 simulate are not yet in the game:
- **The perspective lens** (fog of war): each realm's knowledge of the map (M3.1) shown as it believes it to be.
- **The population density lens.**
- **The settlement inspector's full detail:** buildings with their condition, and the settlement's history.
- **"Run to a chosen year"** among the time controls (only the lab had it).

Later milestones' items (war, government, rulers, claims, vassals) come with them.

**Observable outcome:**
- **The perspective lens.** "View as the X" on the realm panel (a civilization or a tribe) shows the map as that people knows it:
  - **Unknown regions:** under dark fog.
  - **Remembered regions:** muted, in the colour of the people that held them when last seen.
  - **Regions in sight:** in full colour and current state, as the chosen lens shows them.
  - **Selecting a region** says how the people knows it: in sight, last seen in year N (and held by whom then), or unknown.
  - **Settlements, roads, trade routes and bands** are drawn only where the people can see.
  - **Leaving it:** a chip in the lens row says whose view it is and ends it.
- **The population density lens:** each region coloured by its people per km², with a key.
- **The settlement inspector** (the realm panel's Region tab), for each settlement:
  - its tier, townspeople and housing;
  - its buildings with their condition, and those being built with the share paid;
  - its wonder;
  - its own history (its latest events).
- **Run to a year:** the time bar takes a year and runs the history to it, as the lab does.

## Acceptance

- **Tests:**
  - **The perspective:** the frame's perspective for a people lists exactly the regions it has in sight and the regions it remembers, with the occupant and year of each snapshot.
  - **Density:** the lens's values come from the frame's populations and the regions' areas.
  - **The contract:** the perspective is validated.
- **Browser:**
  - "View as" fogs the regions the realm does not know; the canvas counts the fogged, remembered and seen regions as the frame reports them;
  - selecting a remembered region says when it was last seen;
  - the chip ends the perspective;
  - the density lens colours the map;
  - the Region tab lists buildings with their condition and a settlement's history;
  - "Run to year" moves the date to that year.
- **The complete `npm run check` passes.**

## Decisions and defaults

- **Frames** (protocol 38) carry `perspective` when a frame or control asks for `view=<polity>`. It holds:
  - the regions that people has in sight;
  - the regions it remembers, with the snapshot's occupant (−1 for no one) and the year it was last seen;
  - the names of those occupants.

  It is read from the people's own map knowledge (M3.1, `polity.map`); `null` when none is asked or that people is gone. The parser checks:
  - regions of this world, ascending, each in sight or remembered;
  - snapshots no later than now;
  - every remembered people named.
- **The perspective lens** is a mode over the chosen lens, as VISION.md's "view as" toggle:
  - **Regions in sight:** drawn as the lens shows them.
  - **Remembered regions:** muted toward grey in the colour of the people living there when last seen, whatever the lens (the snapshot holds only who lived there); under a grey veil where no one lived.
  - **Unknown land:** under dark fog.
  - **Marks:** bands, settlements, roads, trade routes and cultivated fields are drawn only where it sees. A trade route is drawn only when both its hubs are in sight, even the viewer's own (a frame's routes do not name their parties).
  - **The canvas** reports the regions in sight, remembered and fogged (`data-perspective`).
  - **Selecting land** says how that people knows it.
  - **Not done:** the stale roads VISION.md mentions are not drawn for remembered land, since the snapshot holds no roads, and the panels keep the observer's true view.
  - **Switching:** "View as the X" on the realm panel (a civilization or a tribe) starts it; the chip under the lenses ends it.
  - **A new history** (a reset, or another world) ends it: polity ids start again, so the same id would be another people.
  - **A chosen story's highlight** keeps to the land in sight; fog and memories stand over it.
- **The density lens:** people per km² of each region (`regionDensities`: the frame's population of its people over the region's area). It is coloured on a logarithmic scale from 0.01 (a forager's land) to 300 (the crowded heartlands of a late history), dark violet to yellow, brighter the denser.
  - **Why the range:** at year 1,000 on seed Chronicle a tenth of the regions held under 0.006 per km² and the densest 26.
  - **The key:** its labels sit at their places on that scale.
  - **The canvas** reports the densest region (`data-densest`).
- **The settlement inspector** (Region tab), for each settlement:
  - its tier, townspeople against housing, and founding year;
  - its wonder (standing, or being built with the share paid);
  - its buildings with their condition when below full, and those being built with the share paid;
  - its own history, its latest six events.
- **Run to a year:** the time bar takes a later year and runs to it (the same control as the lab's). While it runs, its button says where it is running to, at a fixed width so the bar does not reflow. The top bar takes two rows at 1,280 and 1,440 px wide.

## Review

The independent review (an agent) checked every living people's perspective at year 1,000 on seed Chronicle (218, all valid, the largest 11.8 KB in a 380 KB frame) and the `view` parameter through the routes. Its findings, all resolved:
1. **Cultivated fields showed through the fog (material),** giving away where people live. Fields are now drawn only in sight.
2. **"View as" outlived a reset or a world change (material):** polity ids start again, so the map showed another people under the old name. A new history now ends it.
3. **Acceptance not asserted (material):**
   - **Remembered land:** the browser now selects a remembered region and checks its note ("Last seen by … in year …, held by … then").
   - **Density:** it checks the densest region against the frame and the region map, and a unit test checks every region's density.
   - **Buildings:** it checks a capital's buildings with their condition.
4. **Smaller fixes:**
   - **Remembered empty land:** veiled.
   - **The chip:**
     - it no longer says "gone" while the first frame is on its way;
     - it names the people from the frame;
     - its live region is the text only.
   - **Buttons:** "View as" keeps one label (pressed or not), so there are no two "Leave their view" buttons.
   - **Comments:** a misplaced schema comment is moved.
   - **Route tests:** they cover `view`.
   - **Density range:** it starts at 0.01.
   - **The run button:** it keeps its width while running.
   - **The density lens:** it fades the rest around a chosen story.
   - **Run:** it is disabled until a later year is entered.
   - **The run status:** it sits outside the button, which had lost its accessible name with the live region inside it (found in the browser).
