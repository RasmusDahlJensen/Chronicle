# M1 — Bands, food and movement

**Feature / milestone / status:** VISION.md milestone M1, delivered as one slice. Status: verified, awaiting user review (2 October 2026); committed on `main` and tagged `civ-m1`; not pushed, so CI has not run.

**Observable outcome:** thirty bands appear at year 0 in attractive, well-watered regions, forage, hunt and fish, deplete game, are born and die from their food security, and spread over each landmass by moving and splitting. The lab shows band markers sized by population, a world population chart, the chronicle's band events with their causes, and, for a selected region, its capacity, game stock and food by method and its band's population, births and deaths, food security and store.

**Relevant spec sections and agreed decisions:** VISION.md "Food, population and borders" (starting state, production, population, land pressure, band movement and fission, settling), "Core loop and time" (store before Pottery), "Data model" (food potential by method), rules 5 and 8, M1 acceptance, story health. User feedback in memory: growth is limited by what the land can feed, with graded trade-offs rather than hard cutoffs.

**Scenario, inputs, and expected result:** the five study seeds at Large run 3,000 years in the headless study; by year 500 population, bands and occupied regions have grown many times over, every band of at least 50 people has births and deaths every year, no region stays above 1.1× capacity for more than 24 months, at least half of all moves cite game depletion or land pressure, and the water-region population share is at least 1.25× the water-region land share.

**Boundary/failure cases and invariants:** at most one band per region; a band with no free neighbour stays and feels the pressure; splits and moves only go to land that feeds the newcomers; bands that die out free their region; every tick, each region's population change equals births − deaths (natural, famine) + migrants in − migrants out, and each band's food store change equals production − consumption − spoilage + carried in − carried out, exactly (integer people, integer food units of 1/100 person-month).

**In scope / out of scope:** in: everything in VISION.md M1, with settling rules recorded for M2 (bands keep the tick they arrived in their region). Out: knowledge, herding and farming, settling (M2); migration between populated regions, civilizations (M3); culture drift and descent of names (M4); raids (M6).

## Decisions and defaults (rule 6)

- **Food model** (`src/simulation/food.ts`, numbers in `FOOD_TUNING`). Per region and method: labour capacity L (people) and yield p (food per worker-year at low labour); output p × L × (1 − e^(−w/L)); workers fill methods to a common marginal yield (closed-form water-filling). Foraging L = Σ cell area × biome density (0.01–0.1 people/km²) × (0.3 + 0.7 × fertility); hunting L = Σ cell area × biome density + 250 per game site; fishing L = shore cells × 80 (coast) / 60 (lake) + river cells × 15 / 30 / 60 by tier (all scaled by cell edge length) + 400 per fish site, plus fishing grounds of 500 for a coastal region, 400 for open-lake access and 300 / 600 for a river or great river (a flagged water region otherwise could have almost no fish). Yields: foraging 1.45, hunting 1.75, fishing 2.0. Game multiplies hunting fully and foraging down to 0.6. Food per person at low density therefore favours water, and capacity — the population whose output equals its need — is 0.05–0.15 people/km² inland and more near water (median region ≈ 2,700 people on Chronicle).
- **Game stock** per region: dG/year = 0.05 G(1 − G) + 0.008 (1 − G) − 0.08 × effort × G, effort = (hunters + 0.3 foragers) ÷ (hunting L + 0.3 foraging L). It "regenerates slowly": a band that over-hunts lowers its land's capacity over decades, which then becomes land pressure and game depletion causes. Earlier, faster settings (0.2 / 0.3) let capacity fall within two or three years, faster than any population could follow.
- **Food security and vital rates** (`POPULATION_TUNING`): FS = (store + 12 × monthly production) ÷ annual need. Births 3.5%/year ± 1.5% as FS goes from 0.2 to 1.8; natural deaths 3.5%/year; famine deaths 1.5 × √(1 − FS) below FS 1. Growth is zero at FS 1. Measured (review): small bands near water reach FS ≈ 1.9 (fishing yields 2.0), so they grow about 1.5%/year, and world growth is 1.44%/year in the first century; inland foragers grow about 0.8%/year. That is above VISION.md's starting value of about 1% for foragers and is flagged for M2 (see known gaps). A population cannot linger above what its land feeds: on flat food curves even a 20–30% overshoot costs only a few percent of food, so a linear famine response left regions above capacity for years.
- **Store before Pottery:** at most one month of need, and a quarter of what is left perishes each month (VISION.md: "small perishable store"). A non-perishing store let bands live off a month's food for two years at a small deficit.
- **Fission** (`BAND_TUNING`): desire = 0.7 × size factor (from 0.6 to 1.1 × the 350-person split size) + 0.3 × land pressure; yearly chance = desire × 0.8; 40% leave for one of the three free neighbours that feed the most people (capacity, less river-crossing cost), weighted by score², if the land feeds them now. The daughter gets a new culture (values ±0.05, same language) and 40% of the store. VISION.md's "about 400" split size became 350 with an earlier start, so people spread over at least half of each settled landmass by year 700 (story health).
- **Movement:** land pressure rises from 0.6 to 1.2 × capacity; a move's desire = food-per-person gain − 0.15 − river-crossing cost (0.1 river, 0.35 great river), only to free land neighbours that feed the whole band; yearly chance = desire × 1.5 × (0.3 + 0.7 × max(pressure, game depletion)). Moves and splits are decided once a year per band, staggered by band id. Reach is foot only: land neighbours on the same landmass.
- **Spawn** (`SPAWN_TUNING`): 30 bands of 50–200 people, weighted by capacity × (1 + 1.0 if near water) × mild-climate factor (Gaussian around 17 °C, width 12 °C), at least 700 km apart; each with its own culture (random values in 0.15–0.85) and language seed.
- **Causes:** band events record their drivers, strongest first (weaker than 0.05 dropped, the strongest always kept): moves — opportunity (relative food gain) and the push contributions of land pressure and game depletion; splits — size and land pressure as they contributed to the desire, game depletion as context.
- **Names** (`names.ts`): each culture has a small phoneme inventory; names have two or three syllables, consonant clusters only at the start and codas only at the end. Naming draws from its own stream at spawn, so naming rules never change where bands start.
- `SIMULATION_RULES_VERSION` 2.

A move's causes are recorded as contributions: the food gain pulls (opportunity, the relative gain), and land pressure and game depletion push in proportion to their share of the move chance. "Citing" means a push contribution of at least 0.1; "led by them" means a push factor is the strongest cause. Game depletion dominates the push: while land is free, bands split long before they fill their region (median size ÷ capacity ≈ 0.26 at year 500), so land pressure is rare until a band is enclosed, and enclosed bands have nowhere to go. Spread is driven by size (fission); the land steers where bands go.

Required: births and deaths every year (0 silent band-years ✓), population grows over 500 years (✓), bands and occupied regions ≥ 3× (✓), ≤ 24 months above 1.1× capacity (✓), every move and split an event with causes (✓, also asserted in tests), ≥ half of moves citing depletion or pressure (✓), water ratio ≥ 1.25 (✓). The same checks run in `tests/simulation-bands.test.ts` on `Chronicle` for 500 years.

Minor ones resolved: the silent-year check now runs in December, so the last simulated year counts; causes break ties by code point; the split's culture and name stream has its own key; `SIMULATION_PROTOCOL_VERSION` is 2 for the new frame fields; markers draw on every world copy and the selection outline stays above the overlay; a stale ARCHITECTURE line and a vacuous test assertion were fixed. Recorded, not changed: the capacity cache is warm-started and differs from a cold solve in the last bits (probed: identical histories over 1,000 years; slice P must persist it or make capacity a pure function); the famine curve is steep (a 10% shortfall kills about 47% a year), which will make environment shocks harsh in later milestones; performance uses about 30% of the budget (two yield solves per band and tick, a capacity solve per band and month, a food-flow object per band and tick), worth trimming before M3–M5; the map stops changing once the land is full (about year 1,200–1,650), expected before M7 and worth showing at the M2 stop.

**Known gap for M2:** VISION.md's M2 criterion compares world growth in the 300 years after a quarter of polities know Agriculture with the 300 years before (≥ 3×). With forager growth near 1.4%/year early and 0.5–1%/year until about year 700, that needs Agriculture to spread after foragers have mostly filled their land, and farming growth well above forager growth. M2 tunes this.
