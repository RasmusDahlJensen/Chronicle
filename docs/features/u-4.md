# U.4 — The ledger

**Feature / status:** the fourth slice of milestone U (the plan is in the [U.1 brief](u-1.md)). Status: verified, awaiting user review (VISION.md rule 2).

**Why:** in the chosen design (A + B), the data views of the "Observatory" mockup (C) become a ledger: world-over-time charts, a table of realms and the treaty web. VISION.md's observer views also ask for a diplomacy lens: with a realm selected, the map coloured by each people's standing with it.

**Observable outcome:**
- **The ledger:** the top bar switches between the map and the ledger. The ledger fills the stage with:
  - **The world over time:**
    - the world's people and polities year by year;
    - the number of civilizations;
    - the great powers' people from the census, each in its colour;
    - the ages marked along the years.
  - **The table of realms:** every civilization (up to 100):
    - its people, regions and settlements;
    - era, faith and culture;
    - treasury, mean stability and unrest;
    - trade agreements, pacts and allies.

    It sorts by any column. Choosing a realm returns to the map at its capital with its realm panel open.
  - **The treaty web:** the civilizations at their capitals on a small world map, joined by their treaties: alliances, pacts, trade agreements and joint roads, each its own colour. Choosing a realm shows its own treaties and names its partners.
- **The diplomacy lens** (a lens on the map). With a realm selected, every other people is coloured by its standing with it:
  - allied, pact, trade partner, rival, neutral;
  - unknown (not met) in dark grey.

  The realm itself is drawn in brass. With no realm selected the lens says to choose one.

## Acceptance

- **Tests:**
  - **The table:** its rows match the simulation (people, regions, settlements, treasury, stability, unrest, agreements and allies per realm).
  - **The treaty web:** it lists exactly the active treaties between living civilizations.
  - **Standings:** the selected realm's standings with every people it has met match the diplomacy rules; unmet peoples are absent.
  - **The contract:** frames carrying these are validated.
- **Browser:**
  - the ledger shows the charts, a table whose rows sort, and the web;
  - choosing a row returns to the map with that realm selected;
  - the diplomacy lens colours the map by standing (counts of regions per standing on the canvas).
- **The complete `npm run check` passes.**

## Decisions and defaults

- **Frames** (protocol 36):
  - **The civilization list's rows** add the table's columns:
    - settlements;
    - ruling culture and state religion;
    - treasury, mean stability and regions in unrest;
    - trade agreements, joint-road partners, pacts and allies.
  - **`treaties`:** the web, one entry per pair of living civilizations and kind of treaty in force, with the number of trade agreements.
  - **`census`:** the census over time, thinned to 300 years. It holds each year's count of civilizations and the people of the eight largest realms now.
  - **The inspected civilization's `standings`:** every living people it has met, as `{ id, status }`. Opinions are formed yearly, so a people met this year is neutral until then.
    - **Why objects:** an earlier `[id, status]` tuple failed in the host's response serializer (fast-json-stringify) with an enum inside a tuple, so every frame for a selected civilization was refused. Found in the browser.
  - **The parser checks:**
    - the web names living civilizations, the lower id first, each pair and kind once;
    - the census series has one value per year for each realm, in order.
- **The ledger:**
  - **Where it lives:** a view over the map, switched from the top bar (Map, Ledger), so the map keeps its place underneath.
  - **The panels:** while the ledger is open the place, story and chronicle panels are hidden.
  - **Choosing a realm** (in the table or the web) returns to the map at its capital, which opens its realm panel.
- **The charts** are plain SVG, with no chart library:
  - the world's people;
  - the great powers (the eight largest realms' people from their census, each in its map colour), on their own scale so they are not flattened under the world's line;
  - peoples and civilizations.

  Each marks the ages.
- **The table** sorts by any column (numbers largest first on the first press). Its realm names are buttons; the column headers carry `aria-sort`.
- **The treaty web:**
  - **The drawing:** the largest civilizations (the frame's list of 100) at their capitals, over the regions' centres as land. Treaties are coloured by kind; a trade line thickens with the number of agreements. A treaty across the date line is drawn the short way round.
  - **Choosing a realm** (from a list or by clicking its circle) shows only its treaties and names its partners by kind.
- **The diplomacy lens** colours each region by its people's standing with the selected civilization:
  - the realm itself in brass;
  - allied, pact, trade partner, rival and neutral;
  - not met in dark grey.

  With a tribe or nothing selected, all is grey and its key says to select a civilization. The lens follows the realm in the place panel. The colours are a data palette (`STANDING_COLORS`), each a hue of its own and a lightness of its own (CIE L* 21 to 82). The canvas reports the regions of each standing (`data-standings`).

## Review

The independent review (an agent) compiled the frame schema with Fastify's own serializer compiler. It serialized and re-parsed 28 real frames (years 550–1,200, with and without an inspected civilization) without a failure. It measured:
- **Frame cost:** 6–14 ms per frame with an inspected civilization.
- **Frame size:** about 16 KB more at year 1,200, in a 422 KB frame.

Its findings, all resolved:
1. **The browser's lens check could hardly fail (material):** only the realm's own count was real. It now asserts every standing's count, computed from the realm's frame.
2. **Peoples met this year showed as not met (material):** standings came from opinions, which are formed yearly. They now come from every people met (neutral until judged), and the unit test checks them against everyone met, including one met just now.
3. **Smaller fixes:**
   - **Story brass:** a chosen story's empty land is no longer drawn in brass under the diplomacy lens (brass is the realm there).
   - **Palette:** its lightness values are spread and stated.
   - **Great powers chart:** each realm starts at its first census instead of a false zero.
   - **React keys:** chart keys are ids.
   - **The lens:** it follows the place panel's realm, not a lagging frame.
   - **The web:** its land is drawn once per world.
   - **Accessibility:**
     - the lens key is a labelled group;
     - the map is inert under the ledger;
     - choosing a realm returns focus to the map.
   - **The table:** its header stays in view.
   - **Wording:**
     - the "Trade" column is "Trade agreements";
     - the table says when it shows only the 100 largest;
     - the census caption allows for thinning.
   - **Tests:**
     - census thinning (601 censuses);
     - a tribe's empty standings.
