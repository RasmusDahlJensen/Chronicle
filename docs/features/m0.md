# M0 — Simulation foundations

**Feature / milestone / status:** VISION.md milestone M0, delivered as one slice. Status: verified, awaiting user review (2 October 2026); committed on `main` and tagged `civ-m0`; not pushed, so CI has not run.

**Observable outcome:** the lab divides the land into simulation regions (a Regions overlay and a region summary in the cell inspector) and shows a History panel with the simulated date, time controls (play, pause, single-month step, speed presets, run to year, reset) and a raw chronicle list. A deterministic monthly tick loop with the twelve systems in VISION.md's order runs in a dedicated host worker per world. `npm run study:history` runs 3,000 years for each study seed through the same worker and reports per-system timing.

**Relevant spec sections and agreed decisions:** VISION.md "Core loop and time", "Data model" (region generation), "The Chronicle", "Observer views" (time controls), "Architecture and engineering constraints", "Headless study", "Tests", M0 acceptance, rules 3–5 and 8. ARCHITECTURE.md hosting and transport boundaries.

**Scenario, inputs, and expected result:**

- Seed `Chronicle`, Large: about 2,500–4,000 land regions of 20,000–60,000 km²; every land cell in exactly one region; at least 90% of non-island regions within the range and none outside it except islands.
- Lab: Reset shows Year 0 · January; Step shows February; Play advances the date until Pause; 10 years/s advances much faster than 1 month/s; Run to year 300 stops at Year 300 · January; Reset returns to year 0. Regions draws region borders; selecting a cell names its region.
- Headless: the same world and seed give identical event-log and state hashes; 3,000 years per study seed complete with per-system timing.

**Boundary/failure cases and invariants:** islands below the minimum area stay single regions; a landmass just above the maximum splits; lakes are water, not region cells; a host restart or another seed resets the simulation to year 0 and the lab says so after a restart; a crashed or evicted worker is replaced at year 0 with a new run id rather than serving stale state; invalid controls are rejected with 400 and wrong methods with 405; simulation requests have their own admission limit.

**In scope / out of scope:** in: everything in VISION.md M0. Out: bands, food and population (M1); any system behaviour beyond the chronicle flush (systems run in order and are timed, but are empty); persistence (P).

## Decisions and defaults (rule 6)

- **Region partition** (`src/simulation/regions.ts`): weighted Poisson-disk seeds (weight 1 + 3 × fertility + 2 near rivers or open lakes) spaced 0.9 × √38,000 km ≈ 175 km by great-circle distance; capped multi-source Dijkstra over four-neighbour steps weighted by true ground length × mean biome movement cost (grassland 1 … desert 2.5, mountain 4, snow 6); enclosed leftovers get their own seed. Repair: an undersized region merges into a neighbour when the result stays ≤ 60,000 km², otherwise takes border cells from neighbours that stay in range and connected; only if neither works is a merged region split by graph distance from its two ends. Island = landmass below 20,000 km² (whole landmass is one region). Regions are renumbered in row-major order of their first cell. The partition's randomness is keyed by the world key, not the simulation seed.
- **River tiers** from the measured runoff distribution of river cells on the five study seeds (Large): median 5,200–5,900, 75th percentile 8,800–10,200, 95th 24,700–30,400, 99th 51,200–74,500, maximum 120,000–275,000. Tiers: stream ≥ 5,000 (every mapped river cell), river ≥ 10,000 (roughly the top quarter), great river ≥ 40,000 (roughly the top 2–3%, the main stems).
- **Region attributes precomputed now** (VISION.md lists them under region generation): coast, open-lake access, river tier, assigned resource sites (land sites, plus fish sites within 2 cells of a coast or lakeshore to the nearest region), defensibility (relief and rough cover, 0–1), land neighbours with travel km (centroid distance × mean terrain cost) and the largest river tier along the border, sea crossings up to 900 km (water Voronoi from coasts), and five ranked settlement sites. Food potential per method is left to M1, which is its first user and tunes it.
- **Clock:** tick 0 is Year 0 · January; a frame shows the month about to be simulated. Speeds: 1, 12 and 120 months per second, and as fast as possible; run to year plays at full speed and pauses in January of that year; the end of the run is year 5,000. The worker runs ticks in slices of at most 40 ms.
- **Live simulations:** one worker per world, at most 2 at once (`CHRONICLE_SIMULATIONS`; the browser test servers use 8 because their scenarios run in parallel on several worlds); the least recently used stops and restarts at year 0 when requested again. Polling: every 250 ms while playing, every second while paused (a paused simulation changes only through controls).
- **"Choosing another seed resets the simulation to year 0"** (VISION.md world-instance identity) is implemented literally: when a lab tab replaces its displayed world with another seed, it resets the chosen world once it is shown. Opening the page attaches to the running simulation without resetting it, so several tabs can watch one history. Manual resets increment a frame `epoch`; worker replacements change the `runId`; the panel remembers the last run id per world in session storage, so after a development reload it can say the simulation started again.
- **Event types:** VISION.md's minimum set is defined now in `shared/simulation.ts` with a template for each in `src/observer/events.ts`; no system emits events yet.

**Independent review** (separate agent, read-only, with reproduction scripts). Material findings and resolutions:

1. *The host leaked failed workers, and concurrent attaches after a failure could create two replacement simulations.* A failed worker is now terminated immediately; `attach` re-reads the live entry after every await, so concurrent observers share one replacement (test: crash with a request in flight, then two concurrent attaches).
2. *Control replies carried no events but advanced the cursor, so events caused by a control would be lost.* Controls now send the cursor and reply with the events from it.
3. *A crash produced a 500 with a terrain message, and a world that cannot start was rebuilt on every poll.* Failures are `SimulationUnavailableError` (503); setup failures are refused for 30 s; the worker reports `failed` before its error reply; a worker that misses a reply deadline is retired (tests: crash → 503-class rejection; backoff without re-decoding).
4. *The determinism test could not fail.* The state hash now includes the partition digest and seed; a new test runs two independent workers on the same world and compares state hash, event log, partition statistics and events. The event-producing systems arrive in M1, which extends the test.
5. *The simulation worker itself could import world generation.* A file-specific Biome rule with a negative case.
6. *A manual reset was invisible to other observers.* Frames carry a reset `epoch`.
7. *Returning to a recently viewed seed resumed it, contrary to VISION.md.* Choosing another seed in the lab now resets the chosen world (see decisions).
8. *Inline tunables in `regions.ts` and unvalidated tunables.* Moved to `REGION_TUNING` (defensibility, settlement-site weights) and validated with the clock and seed weights.

Minor findings also resolved: partition statistics are now part of the worker report and the study's story-health file (the table above is reproducible with the study command); an admission-limit test (503 OVERLOADED, slot released after a failure); `checkPartition` now checks contiguity, landmass membership and the area range, so a repair that silently ran out would fail at startup; the stream-reuse trap is documented on `TickContext`; run-to waits end when a control interrupts the run; late control replies for another world are ignored; the speed test requires at least one month at 1 month/s; one observer disconnecting no longer cancels a shared simulation start; Play is disabled at the end of the run; the Poisson spacing uses the narrower poleward row (partition statistics unchanged). Deferred: sea links only join regions whose nearest-coast water areas touch, so a farther shore behind another region's water gets no link; this matters for M2's Sailing reach and is recorded there. Food potential per method is built in M1 as decided above.
