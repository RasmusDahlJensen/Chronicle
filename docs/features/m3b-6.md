# M3b.6 — Harvests, droughts, famine and irrigation

**Feature / milestone / status:** VISION.md milestone M3b, slice 6. Status: verified (6 October 2026) and committed on `main`, not pushed, so CI has not run; M3b continues with M3b.7.

**Observable outcome:**
- **Harvests vary from year to year.** A good harvest fills the stores; a bad one empties them, and the granaries now carry people through it.
- **Droughts strike now and then,** more often in dry lands. A drought may spread to neighbouring regions and lasts one to three years. Harvests there fall, and herds give less. The chronicle records each drought that touches people.
- **Famine is recorded.** When hunger kills a clear share of a region's people within a year, the chronicle says so, with its causes: a drought, a bad harvest, more people than the land feeds, or unrest.
- **Irrigation** (from the Irrigation tech) serves one whole region. It raises the region's farm yield and its lasting capacity, and halves what a drought takes from its harvest. Farming peoples build it, more so after hard years.
- **The region inspector** shows the last harvest, any drought and any famine.

## Decisions and defaults (rule 6)

- **Weather** (`environment.ts`; `ENVIRONMENT_TUNING`):
  - At each harvest month a region draws its weather: 1 + 0.05 × a standard normal draw (the sum of three uniforms, scaled), within [0.8, 1.2].
  - The crops in the field come in at weather × (in drought, 1 − 0.4 × (1 − the irrigation's share kept)).
  - The gain or loss is the food flow `weather` (in production). The invariant holds it within what weather and drought allow; the region is not checked, because a band may move on after its harvest in the same month.
  - Weather is revealed at the harvest. Food security, which looks at the crops in the field, does not see it coming.
  - Capacity, the land's lasting yield, stays without weather or drought.
- **Droughts:**
  - Each January a habitable region not in drought begins one with chance 0.0004 a year (× 4 in arid land).
  - A drought lasts 1–3 years and spreads to each neighbour with chance 0.5.
  - Herds give 25% less in drought, every month (irrigation halves that loss too).
  - It is an event ("Drought strikes region 1234 and 2 regions around it for 2 years, hitting the Ora") when people live in a struck region, citing dry land or weather.
- **First tuning runs (Chronicle, 1,500 years)** had famines everywhere: 22,942 by 1,500 with harvest spread 0.07, drought chance 0.002 and a famine threshold of 2%.
  - Populations sit exactly at what their land feeds and have no stores beyond the year's, so any shortfall becomes deaths.
  - What fixed it is the reserve below, together with smaller weather, rarer droughts and a higher threshold.
- **Farmers keep a reserve** (`reserveShortfall`; `POPULATION_TUNING.reserveMonths` 3). VISION.md defines food security as stored plus expected food over need.
  - **Births** count food security as lower by how far the store falls short of what lasts until the next harvest plus a reserve. The reserve is up to 3 months, as far as the store holds beyond a harvest cycle: 2 months with Pottery, 3 with a granary or two harvests a year. The shortfall counts at most the reserve's share of a cycle.
  - **Deaths** still follow hunger alone.
  - So a bad harvest empties stores before it kills, and a drought (a 40% cut) still brings famine where there is no irrigation.
  - Granaries now resist famine as VISION.md's table says.
- **Famine** (population system, `recordFamine`):
  - A region's famine deaths are summed, fading by 11/12 a month (about a year).
  - At 4% of its people (and at least 50) the region is in famine: one event for the episode ("Famine strikes the Ora in region 1234: 5,120 of 98,000 people die of hunger within a year").
  - The event cites:
    - a drought (its cut of the last harvest, or of herds now);
    - a bad harvest;
    - more people than capacity;
    - unrest;
    - each only at a weight of 0.03 or more; failing all of those, the shortage itself (often new farmers who outgrew their first harvests).
  - The famine ends when those deaths fall below 1% of the people.
- **Irrigation** (`buildings.ts`, unlocked by Irrigation):
  - purpose farming, one per region, in regions with a river or a lake;
  - 8,000, 36 months, 160 a year;
  - farm yield × 1.2 (part of capacity, recomputed when it is built or lost), and half of a drought's harvest loss kept away.
  - **Need:** the share farmed × (0.3 + hardship), sized by the region's farmers (÷ 10,000) instead of its townspeople.
  - A first version without the river-or-lake rule irrigated almost every farming region (1,972 in Chronicle by 1,500).
- **Contract** (protocol 16, rules 13): the inspector's region has `weather` (the last harvest's share, drought months left, famine, irrigation). The lab shows it as "Last harvest".
- **Stats:**
  - droughts begun and famines recorded, regions in drought and irrigated regions;
  - the study adds a table with famines by strongest cause and the centuries of the first 1,000 years in which world population fell (with or without a recorded famine).

- **Famine events per people:** a polity's regions that fall into famine in the same month are one event, set in the worst of them. A drought often strikes several regions at once.
- **Region cache:** each region's building effects (farm, drought, store, spoilage) are cached in state (`refreshRegionBonus`, recomputed when buildings change or a settlement falls to ruin; an invariant). The environment draws harvest weather from one stream per month, and each January's droughts from one more. Profiling showed the per-settlement scans in the hot paths: 57 → 51.5 ms per simulated year without invariants (Chronicle, years 1,500–1,600).
- **M3b.5 review fixes:** recorded in that brief.

  - Famines come every few years somewhere in the world (about 3–5 a year in later centuries), nearly all from droughts on farmland without irrigation.

  - The chronicle holds 73,000–106,000 events by 3,000 (M3b.5: 40,000–59,000).

## Independent review (after the commit)

One general-purpose agent reviewed `28435bd`.
- **No blockers.**
- **Determinism:** identical hashes with and without invariants.
- **Accounting:** the weather flow was exact in 3,180,104 harvests, and no harvest was weather-adjusted twice.
- **Caches:** the region cache and capacity stay consistent through every change.

**Its findings, fixed with M3b.7:**
- **Should-fix: famine killed people who were fully fed from their stores.** A drought cut herd yields in January, food security fell, and famine deaths followed while granaries were full: 42% of famine events, 13% of famine deaths. It contradicted VISION.md's food security ("stored plus expected food").
  - Now, within what their land lastingly feeds (population at most capacity), hunger counts the months of need in store (`fedSecurity`), and so do townspeople.
  - Above capacity hunger still follows the land, so no store lets a people linger above it.
  - A first version that let stores carry everyone broke M1's over-capacity rule: 71 months above 1.1× capacity against a limit of 24.
  - Births follow the land minus the reserve shortfall as before.
- **Should-fix: famine events were too many** (41% followed the same people's last famine within a year) **and drove tier churn,** and tier falls never named famine.
  - A people's regions falling into famine within a year of its last famine now join it (`Polity.famineTick`).
  - Tier falls cite hard years, a famine there now or unrest, and only fewer townspeople when none of those weighs.
  - With the store fix, famines halved.
- **Should-fix: the weather invariant was a range.** The flows now record the share the harvest came in at, and the invariant checks the flow exactly. The environment sets that share for every region at its harvest months, whoever farms there, so the inspector and famine causes read the same harvest.
- **Should-fix: tests.** Added:
  - hunger counting stores;
  - work and wonders given up after waiting 20 years;
  - a paved road superseding dirt works;
  - roads counted only where both ends are seen;
  - coverage needing the Wheel;
  - irrigation raising capacity at once and needing a river or lake.
- **Minor:**
  - **Docs:** irrigation also halves a drought's herd loss (now documented); the README no longer says weather is not modelled; ARCHITECTURE states the region cache rule.
  - **Streams:** January draws from two streams (the brief now says so).
  - **Study:** the population-fall check now compares the century's famine deaths with the fall.
  - **Reserve shortfall:** it is a share of a year's need, so tropical farmers with two harvests a year no longer have their births halved by a 3-month reserve (two silent band-years in Atlas came from that).
- **Tried and reverted:** freeing townspeople only from surplus beyond the reserve being rebuilt, as the reviewer suggested for the higher specialist share. The shortfall swings with the store through every year, so townspeople swung with the seasons: tier changes rose to 134,000–230,000 per seed.
- **Noted, not changed:**
  - the reserve keeps farming populations a few percent below what their land feeds, so townspeople and wealth are higher than in M3b.5;
  - food security does not see a drought coming, so births go on through it;
  - irrigation's need counts herding as farming.
