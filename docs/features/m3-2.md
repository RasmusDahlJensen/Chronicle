# M3.2 — Decisions, expansion and the political map

**Feature / milestone / status:** VISION.md milestone M3, slice 2 of 3. Status: planned; starts after M3.1 is committed.

**Observable outcome:** every six months each civilization weighs expanding, exploring or doing nothing, using only what it knows. A civilization pressed for land expands into the best neighbouring land it can govern. It sends settlers who found a village, or it takes in a tribe's band living there; otherwise that band moves on. Its governance reach, measured in travel cost from its capital, keeps it from spreading far beyond what it can hold, so mountains, deserts and great rivers slow it. Expeditions reveal land beyond sight and can meet new peoples. The lab gets a political lens (civilizations by colour, capitals as stars) and a list of civilizations with their people. The inspector shows a civilization's last decision with its reasons.

**Relevant spec sections and agreed decisions:**
- VISION.md:
  - "Personality and the decision model": needs, the decision step, pick among the top 3, the decision log.
  - "Expansion": no threshold; land pressure × the best candidate's value × Expansionism, minus distance and reach costs.
  - "Migration" (band absorbed).
  - "Knowledge of the world": exploration, and the implementation rule for decision code.
  - "Stability" (governance reach).
  - M3 acceptance: the sole-civilization scenario, the border travel-cost ratio and the import rule.
  - Story health: the decision mix.
- User guidance: nothing is scripted; outcomes are encouraged through graded incentives.

**Plan (to refine when the slice starts):**
- **Pure decision code** in `src/simulation/decisions/`. Under a Biome rule with a negative test, it may import only the query layer's view types, tunables and the RNG. `perception.ts` builds each civilization's view: its own regions and needs, and its known candidate regions with their known occupant and owner, static land value and travel cost from the capital.
- **The decisions system** (`decide.ts`): every 6 months per civilization, staggered by id.
  - Needs: land pressure, hunger, opportunity.
  - Candidates: Expand (best candidate), Explore, Do nothing.
  - It stores the score decomposition and picks by weighted random among the top 3 above a minimum.
  - Decision log: the last steps per polity, and the action mix per century.
- **Governance reach:** a base × the techs' reach multiplier × government (Chiefdom 1 until M7). Travel cost comes from region edges (km, river crossings) over known regions. The cost of land beyond reach rises smoothly.
- **Expansion** (`expansion.ts`, physical):
  - Settlers from the neighbouring own region with the most people found a village.
  - A band living there is absorbed (a graded chance) or moves on to a free neighbour.
  - Region ownership, ledger flows and events (expansion, settlement founded, band absorbed).
- **Exploration:** an expedition reveals regions along the reachable graph within a range set by mobility, as snapshots. It meets the peoples there (an expedition event, and first contacts).
- **Lab:**
  - The political lens (the Peoples control's default, renamed Political), capitals drawn as stars, and a civilization list (frame contract, protocol 7).
  - The inspector shows the last decision and the civilization's reach.
- **Tests:**
  - Decision scoring units.
  - The import rule (negative architecture test).
  - The sole-civilization scenario on Chronicle's largest landmass: gaps between expansions under 50 years while land pressure > 0.5 and governable land remains.
  - Expansion accounting, absorption and displacement, and browser checks.
- **Study:** the decision mix per century, each civilization's longest gap between expansions, and the border travel-cost ratio.
