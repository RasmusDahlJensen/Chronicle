# M3c.5 — Visible neglect

**Feature / milestone / status:** VISION.md milestone M3c, slice 5, added at the M3c review (user, 7 October 2026: "neglect should be visible, not only in the lack of infrastructure but also the look of infrastructure, a bad road should be visibly bad"). Status: verified, awaiting user review.

**Observable outcome:**
- **A strained realm lets its far provinces go unkept.** A strained court spends on its core. A realm whose costs outrun what customary taxes raise lets its farthest regions' buildings, wonders and roads go unkept, more the more strained it is. If even the taxes it has set fall short, it lets more go, from the outside in. It never lets its capital go.
  - Unkept, they wear, and what stays unkept long enough is lost.
  - It builds nothing new where it lets things go.
  - An episode begins as an event and ends after five years of keeping everything up.
- **Neglect shows on the map:**
  - worn roads fade toward the colour of the land, thin and break up, and crumbling ones are faint trails of dots;
  - worn bridges darken;
  - a settlement whose buildings go unkept or are worn is drawn faded with a broken outline;
  - the region inspector says when a region is let go unkept, and the settlement list shows each building's condition.
- **A worn road is slower:** its help fades with its condition (a road worn away is no road). So neglect also makes far provinces costlier to govern.

**Relevant spec sections:** VISION.md "Wealth" (Deficits, as amended at the M3c review), "Destruction" (everything built has a condition), "Roads".

## Acceptance (this slice)

- **Deferral** (tests):
  - a realm short even at its taxes, with no savings beyond its reserve, lets its farthest regions go unkept first and keeps its heartland;
  - their upkeep is not paid;
  - their buildings wear though everything due is paid;
  - savings beyond the reserve cover a shortfall first;
  - the event begins and ends.
- **Roads** (tests): a worn road is slower than a kept one and no help when worn away. The map's look follows the wear (kept, worn, crumbling).
- **Study:**
  - regions let go unkept, worn roads and worn buildings over time;
  - something is lost to neglect in some seeds;
  - M1–M3c gates hold.
- **Browser:** the map counts worn roads and worn settlements, and the complete `npm run check` passes.

## Decisions and defaults (rule 6)

- **When** (`budget.ts` `deferMaintenance`, at the yearly assessment right after taxes are set), with regions ordered farthest from the capital first:
  - **Strain:** its farthest regions go unkept (`state.neglected`) for the year, a share of its regions of round(½ × strain × count). Strain is how far its costs outrun customary taxes and its sites, as a share of that revenue (`BUDGET_TUNING.neglectStrain` 0.5).
  - **Shortfall:** if its costs with everything kept up still exceed its revenue at the rate just set plus a fifth of its savings beyond its reserve, further regions go unkept, from the outside in, until the upkeep saved covers the gap.
  - **The capital's region** is always kept up.
  - **Episodes:** one begins (an event citing the strain and any shortfall) when a region first goes unkept, and ends (an event) after 5 years in a row of keeping everything up (`keptYears`).
  - **First tried:** only the shortfall rule. Taxes usually rise to cover costs, so realms rarely fell short. In Chronicle, realms toggled into neglect 1,377 times over 3,000 years, a year at a time, and almost nothing stayed worn: 0% of road edges below 80%, and 48 buildings lost.
- **No new work where unkept:** its decision view offers no buildings in, and no roads to, regions it lets go.
- **What it means:**
  - upkeep of buildings, wonders and roads in unkept regions is not due (`costsOf` leaves it out; `fullCostsOf` counts everything, for the tax decision);
  - those buildings and wonders decline at 1 ÷ 120 a month (about 2 years to look worn and 10 to be lost; in arrears, unpaid ones wear at the unpaid share ÷ 60 as before), and those roads at 1 ÷ 120 a month (2 years to look worn, 10 to be lost; roads nobody keeps or unpaid in arrears wear at 1 ÷ 240 as before);
  - kept up again, they mend at 1 ÷ 24 a month.
- **Worn roads:** a road's help is its tier's travel factor plus (1 − condition) × (1 − that factor). A road at half condition is half as much help.
- **The map** (`roadLook`, `roadWear`):
  - **kept** (at least 80%): as before;
  - **worn** (40–80%): 45% faded toward dust, 85% as wide, dashes broken;
  - **crumbling** (below 40%): 70% faded, 70% as wide, a dotted trail at 60% opacity;
  - **settlements:** worn ones (their region unkept, or a building or the wonder below 80%) are filled pale with a broken outline.
- **Contract:** protocol 21 (road conditions in percent, a "worn" settlement feature bit, the region's `neglected`, event `neglect`). **Rules:** 19.

## Review (independent agent, 7 October 2026) and fixes

- **No blocking defect.** Determinism, hashing, save-readiness, validation, the contract and the payment flows hold.
- **Fixed:**
  1. **Medium: the inspector's accounts no longer matched what is paid.** A settlement's account now leaves out upkeep its unkept region does not pay, so the realm's sums again equal what it owes (tested with a region unkept).
  2. **Medium: neglect weakened the M3c.4 brakes on growth.** The budget view (expansion strain, union admission, building) now uses the costs of keeping everything up, so letting regions go does not make a realm look less strained.
  3. **Medium: VISION.md's Deficits bullet did not describe the rule.** It now does: half the strain as a share of regions; savings beyond the reserve drawn a fifth a year before more goes; arrears when even that cannot pay; the capital always kept.
  4. **Low:** the neglect event cites a shortfall whenever there was one.
  5. **Low:** works under way in an unkept region wait (and are given up after waiting too long, as when a town is too small); road works through unkept land are given up; no new road runs through it. A road goes unkept when either end lies in unkept land, and its upkeep is saved by letting its farther end go.
  6. **Low:** a capital moved mid-year is kept up at once, and an invariant checks that no capital's region is unkept.
  7. **Low:** settlements are drawn worn only where something stands. Regions with nothing to keep still count as let go: the realm invests nothing there.
  8. **Low: tests.** A three-region realm checks farthest first, stopping once the shortfall is covered, unkept roads (their upkeep left out, wear, mending) and a wonder there. The browser check compares the map's worn roads with the frame's count; it is often zero against zero, because worn roads are rare at any moment.
  9. **Low: documentation.**
     - The README's road sentences are in order again.
     - M6's military edge is worded as refining war strength's military multiplier.
     - The research note flags its secondary figures and gives the sources for M7's removal-risk factors.

The study was rerun after the review fixes; the numbers above are from that run.

- **Neglect is real now:**
  - strained realms let 3–11% of civilization regions go unkept at a time;
  - over 3,000 years 44–275 road edges and 434–2,330 buildings per seed are lost to it, where M3c.4 lost none;
  - unkept regions' roads look worn after about two years and their settlements look worn at once.
  - **Worn roads are rare at any one moment** (at most 0.5% of road edges below 80%): neglect is mostly in far regions with few roads, nothing new is built there, and what stays unkept is lost within ten years. The worn look shows where and while a realm neglects its edges.
- **Calibration** (also in Decisions):
  1. Only a shortfall at the set tax rate let regions go: 1,377 one-year episodes and almost nothing worn in Chronicle.
  2. Strain one for one (`neglectStrain` 1), buildings lost in 5 years: up to 23% of regions unkept (Elsewhere at 2,000) and 2,000–6,800 buildings lost per seed. Libraries and markets were lost, research slowed, and towns flipped again (Aster: 7 tier changes in 100 years).
  3. Kept: half the strain, buildings declining over 10 years, and unkept roads over 10.
- **Gates hold in every seed:**
  - **Borders:** the ratio at 1,000 is 1.097–1.251.
  - **M3:** 20–30 civilizations at 1,000.
  - **M3b wonders:** 3–7 by 2,500.
  - **M1:** silent band-years 0, and the longest stay above 1.1× capacity is 3–10 months.
  - **M2:** growth ×4.1–5.5, and Agriculture invented independently 32–36 times by 1,000.
  - **M3b settlements and fields:** settlements by water or a site 87–93%, and fields regrew in every seed.
  - **M3c.1 tiers:** at most 6 tier changes of one settlement in any 100 years, with 0–1 settlements changing more than 3 times.
  - **M3c budget:** treasuries 1.6–2.0 years of costs; the largest share at most 33% from 1,000 on, with runs above 35% of 0–129 years.
  - **M3c.3 famine:** famine kills less in rich realms at 2,000 and 3,000 in every seed.
  - **Roads and bridges can be checked only where the techs are reached.** Coverage is 100% wherever counted. In Elsewhere no civilization knows the Wheel until 1,699, so nothing is counted at 1,500, and none reaches Engineering by 3,000, so no bridge can be checked. Across slices Elsewhere's Wheel has come anywhere from 1,160 to 1,699. In the other seeds bridges came within 2–11 years of Engineering.
- **Speed:** 79–121 ms per simulated year.
