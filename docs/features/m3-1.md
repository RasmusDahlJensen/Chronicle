## Decisions and defaults (rule 6)

- **Sight:** a polity's own regions, their land neighbours, and the sea crossings its knowledge reaches (as for movement). Range growth from roads and tech arrives with them (M3b and later).
- **Who remembers:**
  - Civilizations keep a snapshot of a region when it leaves their sight: its occupant, its owner and the month.
  - Tribes keep only what is in sight (VISION.md: bands keep their own and neighbouring regions), so a moving tribe forgets what lies behind it.
  - A tribe that settles starts remembering from then on.
- **Meeting:** two polities meet when one sees a region where the other lives. Contact is mutual, so both learn of each other at once. The event names the region and whether it was across the sea.
  - A breakaway tribe knows its parent and everyone its parent knew, with no event; they learn of it too.
- **Sharing:** once a year (the knowledge system's contact cadence), each civilization learns what each polity in its sight sees ("neighbours learn of each other's immediate surroundings"), as snapshots dated now, unless it has a newer view. This adds known regions, not contacts: hearing of a people is not meeting them.
- **Research contacts:** M2's neutral form ("within two regions") now also requires that the two have met.

## Verification and how the user will try it

## Results, known gaps, and user review

Five seeds, 3,000 years (`.chronicle/studies/m3-1/story-health.md`):
- M2 acceptance still holds in every seed. Agriculture first appears in 527–539, always top-quartile farming land with a river or open lake. A quarter know it by 529–543. Growth after it is 4.1–4.9× growth before.
- M1 acceptance still holds: population ×458–655 and bands ×64–85 by year 500, 0 silent band-years, at most 10 months above 1.1× capacity, 89–91% of moves citing depletion or pressure, water ratio 1.44–1.56.
- Eras first reached: Bronze 784–846, Classical 1,722–2,073.
- First contacts by year 1,000: 161–229 per seed (every seed has them; M3 acceptance). Few, because a breakaway already knows its parent's contacts; most first contacts are between different peoples' tribes meeting as they spread.
- A civilization knows 68–76 regions on average at 1,000 (about 4 regions of its own).
- Performance: 27–39 ms per simulated year (five seeds in parallel; 21–27 before). The knowledge system rose from ~5 to ~9 ms per year (sight, sharing). The first version of the map invariants cost ~23 ms per year; their full checks now run yearly per polity, staggered, at ~7 ms per year.

Known gaps: sight does not yet grow with roads or techs (later milestones), and nothing uses remembered land until M3.2's decisions.
