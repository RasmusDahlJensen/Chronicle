# U.3 — The story rail

**Feature / status:** the third slice of milestone U (the plan is in the [U.1 brief](u-1.md)). Status: verified, awaiting user review (VISION.md rule 2).

**Why:** VISION.md's U asks that "the user can follow a history's main stories (who rises and falls, why, where) without reading tables or the decision log". The chosen design mixes the map-first stage (A) with the stories of the "Chronicle" mockup (B): story cards of what is happening now, each with what happened, why and where, and the ages of the world marked by their firsts.

**Observable outcome:**
- **The story rail** (the game's right-hand panel, beside the great powers, which become its second tab). It shows the history's main stories now, the most important first.
  - **The lead:** the story of the age, larger, at the top.
  - **Each card:** its kind, a title, what happened (with the numbers), why (its strongest causes) and the years.
- **The kinds of story, from what M1–M5 simulate:**
  - **Rise:** a realm that grew most in the last century: from how many people and regions to how many, and how (settlers sent out, tribes joined, bands taken in, realms united with it).
  - **Decline:** a realm that lost most since its peak in the last two centuries, or came to an end, and why (famine deaths, its strained realm let go unkept, regions lost).
  - **Union:** realms that joined a larger one in the last century.
  - **A first:** a people that led the world into a new age.
  - **Faith:** a religion founded in the last century, and how many follow it.
  - **Peoples:** a people grown apart from its forebears, two peoples become one, or a people taking up its rulers' ways.
  - **Trade:** the realm with the widest web of trade agreements and joint roads now.
  - **Voyages:** a realm whose ships reached new lands.
  - **Hardship:** a realm that lost most people to famine in the last century.
  - **Wonders:** a wonder completed.
- **Choosing a story shows it on the map:**
  - the map goes to where it happened:
    - a realm's capital (rise, decline, union, trade, first);
    - the city of a wonder;
    - the worst famine;
    - the farthest new land reached;
    - a faith's holy land;
    - a people's region;
  - it selects that land (so a realm's story opens its realm panel);
  - its regions stand out while the rest is dimmed.
  - Choosing it again, or closing the selection, ends the highlight.
- **The ages:** a timeline in the chronicle panel from year 0 to now. Each age begins where a people first reached it, named with its year and people.

## Acceptance

- **Tests:**
  - **Realm sizes:** each civilization's people and regions are recorded every 10 years, and these records match the history.
  - **Rise and decline:** both come from those records and agree with them; a realm that united into another is a union, not a decline.
  - **Each kind of story:** it is built from the history's events and state, with its numbers, causes and regions. The rail lists at most 8 stories, the strongest first.
  - **The ages:** they are the world's firsts by era.
  - **Text:** every kind of story has a text template; story text names peoples, never "#12".
  - **The contract:** a frame's stories and ages are validated, and invalid ones are refused.
- **Browser:**
  - at year 650 the rail shows stories with names and years;
  - choosing one highlights its regions; a living realm's story opens its realm;
  - the great powers remain one tab away;
  - the ages timeline shows the Stone and Neolithic ages.
- **The complete `npm run check` passes.**

## Decisions and defaults

- **The census** (`state.census`, every `CENSUS_YEARS` = 10): the year, and each living civilization's id, people and regions.
  - **Observation only:** no system reads it, so histories are unchanged (the state hash does not include it). It is part of the save-ready state and of checkpoints.
  - **Size:** about 65 censuses by year 650 and 200 by year 2,000, each one row per living civilization.
- **The story builder** (`src/simulation/stories.ts`) is the observer's, like the frame: it reads the state and never changes it.
  - **What it reads:** the census, the chronicle's events of the last two centuries (found by binary search on their ticks) and the state (religions, treaties, firsts).
  - **When:** once per month (cached per state, tick and event count), in under 6 ms at year 2,000 on seed Chronicle.
- **Weighing:** each story is weighed by the share of the world's people it touched, scaled per kind (a famine's deaths ×10, a faith's followers ×0.5, a voyage ×0.3, and so on), and by recency (full weight now, half at a century old). The rail keeps the strongest eight: at most two rises, declines and unions, and one of each other kind.
- **Rise:**
  - **The measure:** people now against the first census of the last century holding the realm, at least a quarter more.
  - **The accounting:** the gain is split into the people who joined it (tribes, bands and realms, from their events) and its own growth (births over deaths, and migrants). Those two shares are its first causes, then the causes of the events that grew it.
- **Decline:**
  - **The measure:** people now (0 if it ended) against its highest census in the last two centuries, at most four fifths of it.
  - **Why:** famine deaths and regions let go unkept since the peak, with the causes of its hard events.
  - **Unions:** a realm that ended by joining another is told as a union instead.
- **The other kinds:**
  - **Union:** the unification events of the last century, by the realm joined.
  - **A first:** the newest age, if it began within the century.
  - **Faith:** a religion founded within the century, by its followers now.
  - **Peoples:** the largest culture split, hybrid or assimilation of the century.
  - **Trade:** the realm with at least two active trade agreements and the most agreements and joint roads.
  - **Voyages:** the voyages to new lands of the century, by realm.
  - **Hardship:** the famines of the century, by people.
  - **Wonders:** a wonder completed within the century.
- **The ages** come from `state.firsts`: each era after the Stone age starts with the first tech of that era any people learned.
- **Words:** the browser writes each story from its kind's title and text templates (`src/observer/stories.ts`), filled like events' (`fillTemplate`, now shared).
  - **Counts:** `{key|one|many}` writes a count with its noun ("1 region", "3 regions").
  - **Why:** "Why" lists the story's causes in words.
- **Stable stories:** a story's id is its kind and realm (a faith, people, wonder or age adds its own id), so it keeps its id while it goes on. The game follows the chosen story into each new frame by id; it keeps the last copy when the rail no longer tells it.
- **Sects and voyages:**
  - a faith that grew from a schism is told as a sect of its parent faith, with its tenets from the religion itself;
  - a voyage story counts only voyages that reached new lands, not a far coast of land already known.
- **The game page:**
  - **The right-hand panel** has two tabs, Stories (first) and Great powers, using the tab component the realm panel now shares (`src/game/Tabs.tsx`).
  - **A chosen story** moves the map to its place and selects it, highlights its regions and lists its key events ("From the record").
    - **The highlight:** other territories fade to a quarter of their alpha; its land no one holds now is drawn in brass.
    - **The end:** choosing it again, closing the selection or a reset ends it.
  - **The ages bar** sits above the ticker; each age's width is its span of years.
  - **Up to 1,280 px wide**, the chronicle spans the bottom of the window under the selected place and the stories.
- **Frames** (protocol 35) carry `stories` (at most 8) and `ages`. The parser checks:
  - each story is listed once, strongest first, within the history so far and within this world's regions, and so are its events;
  - each later age appears once, in order.

## Review

The independent review (an agent) probed seed Chronicle to year 2,000, building every story every 25 years. It found:
- **Each build:** at most 12.8 ms and 12 KB.
- **No side effects:** the state hash was unchanged by building stories at all 80 samples.
- **The cache:** it never went stale through development controls, resets or loads.

Its findings, all resolved:
1. **Wrong plurals (material):** "1 times" (180 times in the probe), "1 tribes", "1 realms", "1 regions". Fixed with counted nouns; a test writes every kind of story with every count at 1.
2. **Sects told as founded faiths with empty tenets (material).** Fixed (above), with a test.
3. **Story ids moved as time passed (material):**
   - **What happened:** a rise's base census or a union's first event entered the id, so a chosen story lost its mark, its highlight went stale, and choosing it again chose it anew.
   - **Fixed:** stable ids, with the game following the chosen story by id.
4. **Voyages to a far coast counted as new lands (material).** Fixed, with a test.
5. **Smaller fixes:**
   - **Focus:** the map's focus for each kind is now stated in the brief (a living realm's story opens its realm, which the browser test asserts every time).
   - **Region clauses:** they are written only when regions changed ("from 151 to 151 regions" before).
   - **Assimilation:** it shows where it happened, not where the old culture lives.
   - **Tests:** they now check the exact counts and people of rises, unions, famines and trade, and cover a realm that ended and a far-coast voyage.
   - **Layout:** windows up to 1,280 px wide use the bottom-spanning chronicle (the ticker had 277 px at 1,101 px).
   - **Focus rings:** they are no longer clipped.
   - **Lists:** they grow by push, not by spreading.
