# U.2 — The realm panel

**Feature / status:** the second slice of milestone U (the plan is in the [U.1 brief](u-1.md)). Status: planned.

**Why:** VISION.md's "Civ panel" has four tabs (Overview, Diplomacy, History, Knowledge); the game adds Economy. U.1 shows only a realm's summary, so most of what M1–M5 simulate is still reachable only in the lab's inspector. This slice brings it into the game, in words a player can follow: every figure is the simulation's state, and every decision comes with its reasons.

**Observable outcome:**
- **The realm panel** opens when a civilization's land is selected or a great power is chosen. A tribe's land shows the tribe's smaller panel. Its tabs:
  - **Overview:**
    - the realm's name, era, capital, faith, culture, people, regions and treasury;
    - why it acts: its last decision in words, with its reasons and what held it back, and its needs and values as bars;
    - its realm at a glance: settlements by size, its largest cities, its wonders, its stability (mean, and regions in unrest), land let go unkept, and the cultures and faiths of its people.
  - **Diplomacy:** each people it knows, with:
    - its standing (allied, pact, trade partner, rival, neutral) and since when;
    - the opinion and its reasons;
    - the treaties and joint roads between them.
  - **Economy:**
    - taxes, revenue (trades, farms, mines and quarries, trade agreements) and costs (administration, services, upkeep, roads), with the balance and any arrears;
    - each resource against what its techs demand, with shortfalls marked;
    - its trade agreements;
    - its buildings and roads under construction.
  - **Knowledge:**
    - research now: the tech, its progress, why it was chosen and what speeds it;
    - the techs it knows, by era;
    - what it believes of each people it knows (their people and strength) beside the truth;
    - how much of the world it knows.
  - **History:**
    - its origin (the people it descends from, the year it settled);
    - its own chronicle, newest first, in the peoples' names with each event's causes.
- **The region panel:** selecting land shows the region beside the realm. It shows the land and its people (culture, faith, values), its settlements with their sizes, buildings and recent events, the harvest, cultivated land, and its stability with what lowers it.

## Acceptance

- **Browser:**
  - a great power opens its realm panel; each tab shows that realm's state;
  - the Diplomacy tab lists the peoples it knows with standing and opinion;
  - the History tab lists the realm's own events;
  - selecting land shows its region.
- **Tests:**
  - the frame's realm summary matches the simulation (settlements by size, unrest, cultures and faiths add up);
  - a realm's history lists only events it acts in, newest first, kept in step as the history runs and reset with it;
  - beliefs carry the truth beside them.
- **The complete `npm run check` passes.**
