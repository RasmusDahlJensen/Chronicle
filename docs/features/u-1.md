# U.1 — The game shell

**Feature / status:** the first slice of milestone U, "The game UI" (VISION.md). Status: verified, awaiting user review (VISION.md rule 2).

**The direction** (the user's choice, 8 October 2026; [the mockups](https://claude.ai/artifact/RvowjHzM5KdGdAUZMJ6MNy)): A + B mixed.
- **The stage:** a map-first screen (A, "Atlas").
- **The stories:** a story rail of who rises and falls, and why (B, "Chronicle").
- **The ledger:** the data views (C, "Observatory") become a ledger tab.

**U's slices:**
1. **U.1** (this slice): the game shell. The game is the main page: the map is the stage, with a time bar, lenses, the great powers and a chronicle ticker. The developer lab moves to its own page.
2. **U.2:** the realm panel, with tabs Overview (why it acts, its purse, resources, treaties), Diplomacy, Economy, Knowledge and History; and a region panel for the land and its settlements.
3. **U.3:** the story rail. A story builder finds who rises and falls, why and where, from the chronicle and the realms' sizes over time. Choosing a story shows it on the map.
4. **U.4:** the ledger: world-over-time charts, a table of realms, the treaty web, and a diplomacy lens (the map coloured by standing with a chosen realm).
5. **U.5:** the chronicle, filtered by kind with each event's causes, "show on the map", and curated importance (famines summarised, not repeated).
6. **U.6** (added before the close-out, after checking VISION.md's "Observer views"): the perspective and population density lenses, the settlement inspector's full detail, and run to a year.
7. **The U close-out:** review package, independent milestone review, tag `civ-u`.

**Why this slice first:** every later U slice lives inside the shell's layout. The lab's map wiring (tiles, regions, territories, settlements, roads, trade, fields) and its observer polling are shared by both pages, not copied (AGENTS.md: share modules).

**Observable outcome:**
- **The game page.** Opening Chronicle (`/`) shows the world map filling the window.
  - **Top bar:** the date and the leading era; play, pause and step; the speed; and the world's people, civilizations, tribes and settlements.
  - **Lenses:** political, culture, faith, era and descent, with trade routes and roads on or off, and terrain (biomes) when no lens is chosen.
  - **The great powers:** the largest civilizations with their share of the world's people. Choosing one goes to its capital and opens its summary.
  - **Selecting a region** opens a side panel: the realm living there (name, era, capital, people, regions, faith, culture) and the region's own facts.
  - **The chronicle ticker:** the latest notable events, written with the peoples' names (never "#12") and capitalised.
- **The lab page** (`/?lab`): the developer lab as it is today, with its geography layers and testing tools, reached from the game's menu. It links back to the game.
- **One choice of world:** the seed and size from either page; the game keeps its world when the lab is opened in the same tab.

## Decisions and defaults

- **Two pages, one app.**
  - **Addresses:** the game is `/` and the lab `/?lab`. A query rather than a path, because the built app is served as static files without a fallback, so a path like `/lab` would not load in production.
  - **Tests:** the lab's browser scenarios open `/?lab`, and `scripts/review-screenshots.ts` drives the lab there.
- **Shared, not copied** (`src/observer/`):
  - `useObserver`: frame polling with sequence numbers and events since a cursor, and controls. It is taken out of the lab's History panel unchanged.
  - `useWorldMap`: the renderer, detail tiles, selection, and territories, settlements, roads, trade routes and fields from each frame.
  - `useRegionMap`: the region partition.
  - `world-choice.ts`: the world chosen in the tab (session storage), so the game and the lab show the same world.
  - The lab now uses all four.
- **Names in the chronicle.**
  - **The frame:** it carries `names`, the name of every polity acting in its events and in the inspected settlements' events (protocol 33, at most 1,024).
  - **Kept across frames:** `useObserver` merges each frame's names for the run and epoch (`names.ts`), because its event list spans many frames.
  - **The text:** `describeEvent` with `frameNames` writes "the Vraye", and every event begins with a capital ("The Hounan and the Vrihia sign…"). The lab's chronicle and settlement histories use the names too, where they used to print "#12".
- **The look** (the Atlas mockup): a dark atlas with brass accents.
  - **Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond for display and Source Sans 3 for text, both self-hosted from `@fontsource` (OFL-1.1, pinned at 5.3.0), so the game needs no network.
  - **The map:** the renderer takes an optional background colour; the game's is dark, the lab's unchanged.
- **The ticker:** the five newest events of importance 0.1 or more, each with its strongest cause. Curation (famines summarised, not repeated) is U.5's.
- **Realm and region:**
  - Selecting land shows the realm's summary (name, kind, era, capital, faith, culture, people, regions, treasury) and the region's people, settlements and harvest. The full realm panel is U.2's.
  - The great powers are the frame's civilizations by people (the ten largest) with their share of the world's people; one leads to its capital and selects it.
- **Not in the game page** (in the lab, out of the player's way, as VISION.md asks): geography layers (temperature, moisture, fertility), resource sites, the region overlay, run to a year, Load year and scenario triggers.
- **Packages:** `npm audit` reports advisories in the existing development tools' `brace-expansion` and `braces`, not in the new packages; left as they are.

## Review

The independent review compared the extracted hooks with the lab's old code line by line (no regression) and checked contrast (text 15:1, muted text 6.5–7.5:1). Its findings, all resolved:
1. **Names reverted to "#12" within a second (material).** Each frame named only its own events' actors, while the ticker and the lab's chronicle keep events from many frames, so a pact read "The Hounan and…" and then "#3 and #9…" one poll later.
   - **Fixed:** the observer keeps the names of every frame of a run and epoch, and drops them on a reset.
   - **Tests:** a unit test, and a browser check after several polls.
2. **Panels covered the zoom controls, and great-power names were cut short (material).**
   - **What happened:** the zoom controls were covered at 1280×720 and by the ticker on narrow windows; the great powers were hidden on narrow windows; and the era column left 41–65 px for names.
   - **Fixed:**
     - zoom moved into the lens row;
     - the era sits under the name;
     - narrow windows keep the great powers (scrolling) beside the selected place;
     - the ticker is capped at 40% of the map.
   - **Checked:** screenshots at 1024×768, 1280×720 and 1440×900.
3. **Tile and region errors were swallowed (material).** They are now shown with Retry buttons; a click before the regions arrive says "Reading the land…".
4. **Smaller fixes:**
   - **Retries:** a first-load failure offers Retry.
   - **Ticker text:** an empty ticker says "Nothing notable among the latest events".
   - **Names:** a frame names at most 1,024 peoples.
   - **Keyboard:** the map's keys are described to screen readers.
   - **World menu:** it takes focus and closes on Escape, and starting the history again asks for confirmation.
   - **The top bar:** it is a banner landmark with a Time region inside.
   - **The renderer:** its background is one of the renderer's dependencies.
5. **The browser suite overloaded the host (found in the complete check, two runs of three).**
   - **The cause:** the game's scenarios generated a world of their own and ran it to year 750 in both servers. The host's admission control (two compute workers, a queue of four) then refused the lab's world ("The host is busy") in the production tile-failure scenario.
   - **Fixed:** the game's scenarios now share the history scenarios' world and their run to year 650 (`tests/browser/simulation.spec.ts`, serial). The great powers are checked in a second tab while that history stands at 650. No new world and no extra history.
