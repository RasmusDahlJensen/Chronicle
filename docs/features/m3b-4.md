# M3b.4 — Wonders

**Feature / milestone / status:** VISION.md milestone M3b, slice 4. Status: verified (6 October 2026) and committed on `main`, not pushed, so CI has not run; M3b continues with M3b.5.

**Observable outcome:**
- **A few named wonders rise over the centuries:** the Pyramids, the Hanging Gardens, the Colossus, the Great Temple, the Great Library, the Grand Observatory, the Cathedral and the Palace.
- **Each is unique in the world while it stands.** A civilization begins one only with a motive (a pious people, an ambitious one, or a stable and prosperous realm) and the means.
- **Each has its own effect:**
  - the Great Library and the Grand Observatory raise research;
  - the Colossus raises wealth;
  - the Pyramids, the Great Temple, the Cathedral and the Palace steady the realm;
  - the Hanging Gardens house more people in their city.
- **The chronicle records them:** a wonder begun, completed and destroyed are major events, each with its causes.
- **A wonder can be lost.** One in a settlement that falls to ruin is destroyed and may be built again elsewhere.
- **In the lab:** the history panel lists the wonders of the world, settlements show theirs in the inspector, and the map marks them at detail zoom.

**Relevant spec sections:** VISION.md "Wonders", "Data model" (Wonder), the Chronicle event types (wonder begun, completed, destroyed) and the M3b acceptance: "Every building, road and wonder event records its causes; at least one wonder is completed by year 2,500 in most seeds."

## Decisions and defaults (rule 6)

- **Wonders** (`wonders.ts`): tech, motive, cost, months, upkeep (cost ÷ 500 a year), the smallest tier of its city, coast, effect.
  - the Pyramids (Writing; ambition; 2 M; 20 years; stability +0.04 across the realm);
  - the Hanging Gardens (Irrigation; ambition; 1.5 M; 15 years; housing × 1.5 in its city);
  - the Colossus (Bronze working; ambition; 1.5 M; 15 years; by the sea; wealth × 1.1);
  - the Great Temple (Organized religion; piety; 2.5 M; stability +0.05);
  - the Great Library (Philosophy; learning; 3 M; research × 1.1);
  - the Palace (Administration; ambition; 3 M; stability +0.04, housing × 1.3);
  - the Grand Observatory (Astronomy; learning; 4 M; research × 1.1);
  - the Cathedral (Theology; piety; 5 M; stability +0.06).
  - Prestige and cultural influence, and the Great Library's protection against knowledge loss, come with M4 and M7.
- **The choice** (`decisions/build.ts` `bestWonder`, a Build option):
  - need = the motive's value (piety: Zeal, ambition: Expansionism, learning: Openness) × golden age (clamp((mean stability − 0.6) ÷ 0.4)) × greatness (min(1, the largest fitting city's townspeople ÷ 20,000));
  - score = 0.6 × need − the cost and upkeep burdens as for buildings;
  - one wonder at a time per civilization;
  - its city is its largest that fits (tier, coast) and has no wonder;
  - the causes cite the motive and the golden age.
- **Unique in the world** while one stands or is being built (an invariant). A wonder is paid in instalments from its city's owner's treasury, and is kept up and worn like a building.
- **Fate:** a wonder is destroyed when its city falls to ruin ("cityRuined") or it is worn away ("unpaidUpkeep"); then another may be built anywhere. Unfinished work in a ruined city is abandoned. A union passes wonders with their cities.
- **Events:** wonder begun (importance 0.3), completed (0.7), destroyed (0.5), with new templates naming the civilization by name.
- **Contract** (protocol 14, rules 11):
  - the frame lists the wonders of the world (name, city, holder, begun, built);
  - the inspector shows a settlement's wonder or the work under way;
  - the feature mask adds 8 for a wonder (a gold disc above the settlement at detail zoom).
- **Review fixes for M3b.2 and M3b.3** (recorded in those briefs). The M3b.3 blocker was buildings one region needs only one of being begun twice in one decision; it is fixed four ways, with an invariant and a regression test. Also: exploration's fresh sea reach and the expedition range count only once the civilization has a harbor.

## Plan (as written before implementation)

- **Data:** each wonder lists the tech that allows it, its cost, months, upkeep, the smallest settlement tier, and its effect, applied civilization-wide (research, wealth, stability) or in its city (housing).
- **The Build action** weighs at most one wonder at a time, in the civilization's largest city:
  - motive: Zeal for temples and cathedrals, Expansionism for palaces and colossi, Openness for libraries and observatories, plus the realm's stability and its income;
  - against: its cost relative to income and treasury.
- **State:** `state.wonders`, with type, settlement, builder, begun, completed, condition and destroyed. Wonders pass with their settlement in a union, and are destroyed when it falls to ruin.
- **Contract:** the frame lists the wonders of the world; the inspector shows a settlement's wonder; settlements carry a wonder flag for the map.
