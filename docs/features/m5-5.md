# M5.5 — Pacts, alliances and road agreements

**Feature / status:** the fifth slice of M5 (the plan is in the [M5.1 brief](m5-1.md)). Status: verified, awaiting user review (VISION.md rule 2).

**Why:** VISION.md's "Diplomacy and war" names the diplomatic states. M5.4 brought trade agreements; this slice brings the rest that come before war:
- **non-aggression pacts**;
- **defensive alliances**;
- **road agreements:** "allies and trade partners agree to build joint roads between their nearest cities, each side building its own half; warmer relations make this likelier and sooner".

Proposals come from the decision model; the receiving civilization accepts if its own score for the deal is positive. War (M6) will give pacts and alliances their full meaning: a pact partner is not attacked, and allies are called into wars. Until then they already change what a civilization fears, whom it learns from and what it thinks of the other.

**Observable outcome:**
- **Non-aggression pact.**
  - **Who proposes:** a civilization weighs proposing one to a neighbour whose land touches its own. It is drawn by the threat it feels from them and by tension along the border; its Militarism and Expansionism hold it back.
  - **Effect:** the threat it feels from a pact partner is halved.
- **Defensive alliance.**
  - **Who proposes:** a civilization weighs proposing one to a civilization it has met that, as far as it knows, borders a neighbour it feels threatened by: a common threat. A good opinion, kinship and a shared faith draw it.
  - **Effects:**
    - an ally poses no threat;
    - half an ally's believed strength counts beside its own when it judges others;
    - allies research what the other knows faster, as an exchange does (VISION.md "Sharing knowledge": "From M5, trade agreements and alliances also share knowledge").
- **Road agreement.**
  - **Who proposes:** a civilization weighs proposing one to an ally or trade partner whose land touches its own, when both know how to build roads. Opinion and its infrastructure need draw it; the cost of its half holds it back.
  - **The road:**
    - it joins their nearest towns or cities along the cheapest way through their two lands;
    - each side builds the stretch in its own land, at its own best road tier, paying for it as for its other roads;
    - the stretch across the border is built and kept by whoever keeps it (the holder of its first region);
    - a completed joint road is a cross-border road, and it raises the capacity of their trade.
- **Acceptance.** The partner judges with its own score, which must be positive:
  - **its opinion of the proposer;**
  - **for a pact:** its own fear of the proposer, how pressed it is elsewhere, its Militarism and Expansionism;
  - **for an alliance:** its own fear of the common enemy, and kinship and faith;
  - **for a road:** trade or alliance between them and its infrastructure need, against its half's cost.
  - **A refusal** is remembered: the proposer thinks less of them, and does not ask again for a while.
- **Relations.** Pact partners, allies and road partners grow more intimate and warmer. Each treaty is a reason in their opinion.
- **Ending.**
  - **Relations sour:**
    - a pact ends when opinion on either side falls below −60;
    - an alliance below −10;
    - a road agreement below −40.
  - **Other ends:**
    - a party is gone;
    - a road agreement whose road can no longer be built (land on the way changed hands, a city fell) ends with it.
  - Each beginning and ending is a chronicle event with its cause.
- **Lab:**
  - **Diplomacy section:** each people a civilization knows has a status (allied, non-aggression pact, trade partner, rival at an opinion of −50 or below, neutral), its agreements and the year the status began.
  - **Decision text:** the decision names the pact, alliance or road it proposed.

## Acceptance

- **M5:** by year 2,000 every seed has at least one alliance or non-aggression pact. In most seeds, allies or trade partners have built at least one cross-border road.
- **Tests:**
  - only a neighbour is offered a pact, and only a civilization bordering a feared neighbour an alliance;
  - acceptance follows the partner's own score;
  - pacts halve and alliances remove the threat felt, and allies' strength counts;

  - intimacy and opinion rise;

## Decisions and defaults

- **One treaty store.** `state.treaties` holds every treaty, a union by type: trade (M5.4), non-aggression pact, alliance and road agreement.
  - **Partner lists:** each civilization's `pacts`, `allies` and `roadPartners` hold its partners of each kind, at most one treaty of a kind per pair. An invariant keeps them equal to the active treaties.
  - **Lookups:** `treaties.ts` holds the lookups shared by trade, diplomacy and the view.
- **Threat** (`perception.ts`):
  - `fearOf` is the one rule: the need's per-neighbour threat, measured against its strength with its allies (`strengthWithAllies`: half each ally's believed strength).
  - A pact partner's threat is halved; an ally poses none.
  - Acceptance uses the same rule with the partner's own beliefs.
- **Proposing** (DIPLOMACY_TUNING; opinions ÷ 100):
  - **Pact:** 0.5 × the threat felt from the neighbour + 0.08 × its dislike (÷ 50, at most 1) + 0.05 × (1 − Militarism) − 0.06 × Expansionism.
  - **Alliance:** 0.5 × the common threat + 0.15 × opinion + 0.05 kin + 0.05 shared faith − 0.06 × Expansionism.
    - **Candidates:** civilizations it has met and thinks at least 0 of, which its map shows holding land beside a neighbour it fears.
    - **Its target:** the alliance names that enemy.
  - **Joint road:** 0.02 + 0.04 for a trade partner or ally + 0.06 × opinion + 0.1 × its infrastructure need − 0.5 × the cost of its half ÷ revenue.
    - **Its half:** half the great-circle km between their nearest towns or cities, × 1.3, at its tier's cost per km.
  - **Who is listed:** none below the opinion at which the treaty would end, and none to a people that refused it a treaty, or with whom one soured, in the last 20 years.
  - **The decision log** records the believed strength of whom a pact or alliance fears.
- **Accepting** (the partner's own score, positive, and its opinion at or above the ending threshold):
  - **Pact:** 0.05 + 0.4 × its fear of the proposer + 0.2 × the largest threat it feels from others + 0.3 × opinion + 0.1 × (1 − Militarism) − 0.15 × Expansionism.
  - **Alliance:** −0.05 + 0.5 × its own fear of the enemy (none if it has not met them) + 0.3 × opinion + kin + faith.
  - **Joint road:** 0.02 + 0.04 if they trade or are allied + 0.06 × opinion − 0.5 × its true stretch's cost ÷ its revenue.
  - **The event:** it logs each part of the partner's score.
  - **A refusal:** remembered (−5 opinion, half-life 10 years).
- **Joint roads** (`roads.ts` `jointRoute`, `diplomacy.ts`, `construction.ts`).
  - **The way:** between the two civilizations' nearest towns or cities (great-circle), the cheapest route through their two lands. It never passes a region its holder lets go unkept, as a realm's own road works do not.
  - **Who builds:** each edge belongs to its keeper (the holder of its lower region) and is built at that side's best tier, as a road work naming the agreement.
  - **When work stops:** a stretch is abandoned when the agreement ends, either town falls or changes hands, the way leaves their two lands, or a region of its stretch is let go unkept. A side left with no work and no road ends the agreement ("its road can no longer be built").
  - **Completion:** when both sides have built, the cross-border road is complete. It is an event and a metric, and it adds 1 to the capacity of their trade routes.
- **Effects:**
  - **Intimacy targets:** +0.1 for a pact, +0.3 for an alliance, +0.2 for a joint road.
  - **Opinion:** +8, +15 and +5 points, named reasons in the Diplomacy section.
  - **Teaching:** allies teach as an exchange does.
- **Ending:**
  - **Relations sour** (yearly per treaty): opinion on either side below −60 for a pact, −10 for an alliance, −40 for a road. Both sides remember it (−5, half-life 15 years) and propose nothing to each other for 20 years.
  - **Monthly:** a party gone, or the road lost.
- **Contract and lab:**
  - protocol 30: `DECISION_ACTIONS`, and the Diplomacy rows' `status`, `agreements` and `since`; rules version 28.
  - The decision reads "Propose a non-aggression pact with the X", "Propose an alliance with the X against the Y" or "Propose a joint road with the X".
  - Road events say "toward" for one side's half and name the completed joint road.
- **The diplomacy lens** (VISION.md "Diplomacy lens": the map coloured by relation to a selected civilization) is left to milestone U's game UI. The lab shows each people's standing in the inspector.
- **Older tests updated for the new rules, not weakened:**
  - M2's sharing check now counts trade partners and allies as teachers (VISION.md: "From M5, trade agreements and alliances also share knowledge").
  - A civilization's destruction may come with its treaties' endings (still one destruction event).
  - The decision log may record strength beliefs.

## Results

The study is `.chronicle/studies/m5-5` (five seeds, 3,000 years, 8.8 minutes). For iterating: two seeds to 2,000, and a fork from year 800.
- **Every seed has pacts and cross-border roads by 2,000 (M5).**

  | Seed | Pacts · alliances by 2,000 | Cross-border roads by 2,000 / 3,000 | Active at 3,000: pacts · alliances · road agreements |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Chronicle | 77 · 3 | 13 / 92 | 26 · 0 · 14 |
  | Elsewhere | 114 · 4 | 4 / 85 | 35 · 7 · 7 |
  | Atlas | 92 · 8 | 4 / 16 | 23 · 0 · 2 |
  | Verdant | 69 · 2 | 11 / 119 | 27 · 0 · 13 |
  | Aster | 95 · 3 | 34 / 120 | 21 · 0 · 9 |

  - **Endings by 3,000:**
    - "a party is gone" leads among pacts and alliances (45–97 a seed);
    - relations souring is rare (11–17);
    - joint roads are often lost (13–139), worn away when their keepers let the land go unkept or cannot pay, and many are agreed and built again.
  - **Refusals:** 57–160 proposals refused a seed.
- **Trade by 2,000:** 25 / 30 / 21 / 44 / 55 agreements (M5.4: 29 / 31 / 46 / 44 / 43). Atlas's history diverged early.
- **Opportunity need:** it no longer counts pact and alliance partners, and pacts are common, so it fell to 0.00–0.10 at 2,000 (M5.4: about 0.3). No decision reads it until M6.
- **Gates hold:**
  - the longest run above 35%: 78/0/0/0/102 years;
  - civilizations at 1,000: 23/31/16/21/29; at 2,000: 27/30/34/30/25;
  - unions by 3,000: 38/59/49/44/66;
  - hybrids by 2,000: 2/6/3/4/2;
  - splits from a ruling culture by 2,000: 12/19/7/9/14;
  - a religion founded by 2,500 in every seed;
  - decisions citing a belief more than half off: 16–62;
  - the causes rule: 0 short.
- **Speed:** 111–163 ms per simulated year (M5.4: 107–152); decisions carry the joint-road search.
