# M6.5 — Alliances at war, coalitions and vassalage

**Feature / status:** the fifth slice of M6, "War and peace" (VISION.md; the plan is in the [M6.1 brief](m6-1.md)). Status: planned.

**Why:** VISION.md's "Alliances and coalitions": "Defensive allies are called into wars and accept based on their own scores. When one civ's power passes a share of all known power (configurable), polities that know it gain a Threat need against it, so balance-of-power coalitions emerge against would-be hegemons." Its peace terms: "Overwhelming: vassalage, or annexation if the loser is small", and the diplomatic state "tributary or vassal". M5.5's alliances so far only deterred; M6.2's wars were fought by two realms alone.

**Observable outcome:**
- **Allies called in:** when a realm is attacked, each of its allies weighs joining the war on its side (its own score: the alliance, its opinion of both, its war-weariness, the odds); one that joins fights beside it (its strength on the front, its share of the dead and the exhaustion, a place at the peace); one that refuses is remembered for it.
- **Coalitions against a hegemon:** a realm whose power passes a share of all the power another people knows is a threat to that people whatever it thinks of them: alliances against it and wars to cut it down become likelier, so coalitions form against would-be hegemons.
- **Vassalage:** an overwhelming victory over a realm too large to annex makes it a vassal: it pays tribute each year, fights no war of its own and is defended by its overlord, until it is strong enough to stop paying (an event) or its overlord falls.
- **The game:** allies at war in the Wars tab and the ledger; "vassal" and "overlord" standings (the diplomacy lens, the rows, the treaty web); the chronicle: allies joining or refusing, vassalage, breaking free.

## Acceptance

- **Tests:** an ally weighs joining by its own score and joins or refuses (remembered); its strength counts on the front and its dead are exact; the hegemon threat rises with a power share; vassalage at overwhelming terms, its tribute exact, its end.
