# M6.5 — Alliances at war, coalitions and vassalage

**Feature / status:** the fifth slice of M6, "War and peace" (VISION.md; the plan is in the [M6.1 brief](m6-1.md)). Status: planned (next).

**Why:** VISION.md's "Alliances and coalitions": "Defensive allies are called into wars and accept based on their own scores. When one civ's power passes a share of all known power (configurable), polities that know it gain a Threat need against it, so balance-of-power coalitions emerge against would-be hegemons." Its peace terms: "Overwhelming: vassalage, or annexation if the loser is small", and the diplomatic state "tributary or vassal". M5.5's alliances so far only deterred; M6.2's wars were fought by two realms alone.

**Observable outcome:**
- **Allies called in:** when a realm is attacked, each of its allies weighs joining the war on its side (its own score: its opinion of both, the threat it feels from the attacker, the odds, its war-weariness and arrears, a war it fights already); one that joins fights beside it (its strength on the front, its own dead as war deaths, its own weariness) until the peace; one that refuses is remembered for it.
- **Coalitions against a hegemon:** a realm whose power passes a share of all the power another people knows is a threat to that people whatever it thinks of them: alliances against it and wars to cut it down become likelier, so coalitions form against would-be hegemons.
- **Vassalage:** an overwhelming victory over a realm too large to annex makes it a vassal: it pays tribute each year, declares no war of its own and is defended by its overlord, until it believes itself as strong as its overlord and throws it off (an event), or its overlord is gone.
- **The game:** allies in the Wars tab; "vassal" and "overlord" standings (the Diplomacy rows, the diplomacy lens); a realm's overlord and vassals; the chronicle: allies joining or refusing, vassalage, a vassal going free.

## Acceptance

- **Tests:** an ally weighs joining by its own score and joins or refuses (remembered); its strength counts on the front and its dead are exact war deaths; at the peace it stands down weary; the hegemon threat rises with a power share and is felt whatever the opinion; vassalage at overwhelming terms, its tribute exact, no wars of its own, its overlord defends it, its end (strength, overlord gone); the invariants hold.

## Decisions and defaults (planned)

- **Allies called in** (`allies.ts`, when war is declared): each ally of the defender that has met the attacker, is not allied to it too, is no vassal and fights no war with it already weighs joining: 0.1 + 0.3 × its opinion of the defender ÷ 100 − 0.2 × its opinion of the attacker ÷ 100 + 0.3 × the threat it feels from the attacker + 0.3 × (2 × the odds − 1) − 0.3 × its war-weariness − 0.3 × its arrears − 0.3 if it fights a war already − 0.2 if a pact binds it to the attacker; the odds are its strength and the defender's as it believes it against the attacker's. Above 0 it joins; a vassal's overlord always joins. One that joins: its treaties with the attacker end, both remember the war, it sets its army for war. One that refuses: the defender remembers being abandoned (−30, halving every 40 years). Events: `allyJoined`, `allyRefused`.
- **Allies at war** (`war.ts`): each month each ally adds half its strength × its supply to the front to the defender's side (on the defender's ground when the front is there) and loses soldiers at the defender's rate (war deaths, its own count); its weariness grows as a side's exhaustion does but for occupation. Allies are on the defender's side for every purpose: at war with the attacker (no trade, treaties, standing "at war") and in truce with it after the peace. Allies seek no separate peace: they stand down at the peace, their weariness what the war left.
- **Hegemons** (`perception.ts` `fearOf`): a civilization whose believed strength is more than 30% of all the strength a people knows of (its own and every living civilization it has met, at least four of them) is a threat to it whatever it thinks of them: (share − 0.3) ÷ 0.7, the larger of that and the fear it feels anyway; none from an ally. A hegemon it does not border is among its threats too, so alliances against it (M5.5's common threat) and pre-emptive wars on it become likelier: coalitions.
- **Vassalage** (`vassals.ts`; VISION.md's peace terms): a winner at 75 or more over a loser too large to annex, that is no vassal itself, makes it a vassal (instead of taking land): it pays 15% of its revenue a year to its overlord, month by month (wealth flows; its overlord's war income), declares no war (and its overlord none on it), and its overlord joins any war on it. A vassal's own vassals go free (no chains). Each January a vassal whose strength reaches its overlord's as it believes it throws it off (its overlord remembers it, −30, halving every 40 years), and a vassal whose overlord is gone goes free: the event `vassalFreed`. A vassal weighs joining its overlord in a union more (+0.2).
- **The game:** allies in the Wars tab (who joined, their dead) and in the chronicle; standings "vassal" and "overlord" in the Diplomacy rows and lens; the realm's overlord (since when, its tribute) and vassals.
