# M3b.3 — Mines, quarries and harbors

**Feature / milestone / status:** VISION.md milestone M3b, slice 3. Status: verified (6 October 2026) and committed on `main`, not pushed, so CI has not run; M3b continues with M3b.4.

**Observable outcome:**
- **Mineral sites need a mine.** Copper, tin, iron, gold, coal, uranium and salt yield nothing until a civilization that can use the resource builds a mine in a settlement of that region. Stone sites likewise need a quarry.
- **Worked sites earn wealth**, and quarried stone makes stone buildings (walls, temples, aqueducts) cheaper.
- **Sea crossings need a harbor.** A region can be left by sea, seen across the sea and reached for contact only from a region with a standing harbor, under the sea reach the civilization's techs give (Sailing: coastal crossings; Navigation: any coast). Harbors also improve fishing.
- **Tribes cannot sail.** They build nothing, so no tribe crosses the sea.
- Mines, quarries and harbors are drawn on the map at detail zoom and listed in the settlement inspector. The chronicle records each with its reasons, for example "a mine at Kesh: the copper the Ora now know how to use".

**Relevant spec sections and agreed decisions:**
- VISION.md:
  - "Settlements and infrastructure": mines and quarries;
  - "Buildings": harbor, mine, quarry;
  - "Knowledge and technology", Mobility: "Sailing (from a region with a harbor)" and "Navigation (from a region with a harbor)";

  - the milestone neutral form: "until M3b, Sailing and Navigation need no harbor, and mineral sites need no mine".

## Acceptance (this slice)

- No polity's sight, movement, expansion, exploration, contact or travel from its capital crosses a sea link from a region without a standing harbor of its own. The map invariant and the movement tests check it.
- A mineral or stone site yields only with the extraction knowledge and a mine or quarry in its region (unit test).
- Every mine, quarry and harbor completed is an event with its causes.
- M1–M3 and M3b.1–2 gates hold, and the complete `npm run check` passes.

## Decisions and defaults (rule 6)

- **Mine** (unlocked by Mining): 10,000, 36 months, 200 a year.
- **Quarry** (unlocked by Iron working, which gains the unlock: VISION.md puts the quarry in the Iron era): 6,000, 24 months, 120 a year.
- **Harbor** (unlocked by Sailing): 12,000, 36 months, 240 a year. It needs the sea beside the settlement.
- **One of each per region.** A region's mine works all its mineral sites and its quarry all its stone sites.
- **Site yields** (`WEALTH_TUNING.siteYield`, a year per worked site the civilization can use, less in unrest):
  - copper 1,500; tin 2,000; iron 1,500; gold 4,000; coal 1,000; uranium 2,000; salt 1,500; stone 800.
  - Usable follows `depositState`: metals once a tech uses them, the rest once extracted.
- **Stone buildings** (walls, temple, aqueduct) cost 75% where the civilization quarries usable stone. A work records its cost when begun.
- **Build needs** (`BUILD_TUNING`):
  - mining: min(1, the region's unworked usable site yield ÷ 3,000), whatever the settlement's size;
  - sea: a coastal settlement's min(1, sea crossings within the civilization's reach ÷ 3) × (0.2 + (Openness + Expansionism) ÷ 2);
  - purpose weights: mining 0.4, sea 0.35.
- **One sea-reach rule** (`perception.ts` `seaFrom`): a polity's sea reach counts from a region only where it holds the region and a harbor stands there (`state.harbors` counts them per region, an invariant).
  - Used by sight, meeting across the sea, travel from the capital, expansion candidates, exploration steps, band movement and research contacts, and by the map invariant.
  - Harbors built or lost make the owner's sight dirty.
  - Expeditions cross the sea only from the civilization's own harbors; overseas voyages come with M5.
- **Tribes never cross the sea.** They build nothing. Before M3b, tribes with Sailing could move overseas.
- **Not done:** harbors do not raise fishing. A harbor bonus to fish yields would also have to raise the region's capacity, or people would sit above it, against M1's over-capacity rule.
- **Contract** (protocol 13, rules 10): settlement arrays carry a feature mask (1 harbor, 2 mine, 4 quarry). The map draws them beside settlements from zoom 4: a blue ring, a dark triangle, a grey square.
- **Invariants:**
  - Per-settlement building checks (housing, bonus, conditions) now run once a year per settlement, staggered, instead of every month.
  - The treasury ledger and the harbor counts are still checked every month.

## Plan (as written before implementation)

- `buildings.ts`:
  - **mine** (unlocked by Mining), purpose `mining`;
  - **quarry** (unlocked by Iron working, which gains the unlock: the Iron-era stone tools of VISION.md's table), purpose `mining`;
  - **harbor** (unlocked by Sailing), purpose `sea`, on settlements next to the sea;
  - at most one of each per region.
- `economy.ts`: yearly wealth from each worked site the civilization can use (`WEALTH_TUNING.siteYield` by resource), and a stone-building discount where the civilization has a quarry.
- **One sea-reach rule** (`seaReach(state, polity, region)`) used by sight, meeting, travel, expansion candidates, exploration, band movement, contacts and the invariant. Harbors make the map dirty when they are built or lost.
- **Build view:** coastal settlements and their reachable sea links, and the yearly yield of the region's unworked usable sites.
