# M3.3 — Joining and stability

**Feature / milestone / status:** VISION.md milestone M3, slice 3 of 3. Status: planned; starts after M3.2 is committed. M3 is tagged `civ-m3` after this slice, and then the run stops for the user's approval (user request, 2 October 2026).

**Observable outcome:**
- When a farming tribe would settle next to a civilization's land, it weighs joining that civilization against founding its own, and the chronicle says which and why.
- Kin and neighbours who see a large, well-fed, stable civilization tend to join it. Proud, traditional or expansionist tribes tend to found their own. A civilization takes in only people it can govern from its capital.
- Early civilizations grow beyond their first valley as the tribes around them settle into them, so the map consolidates into tens of countries whose borders follow mountains, deserts and great rivers.
- Each civilization region has a stability:
  - It falls with hunger and with distance beyond governance reach, and slightly where a different people was taken in.
  - Unrest lowers output and is an event.
  - Tribes deciding whether to join see the stability of the civilization's regions near them.

**Relevant spec sections and agreed decisions:**
- VISION.md:
  - "Joining".
  - "Stability, fracture and civil war": regional happiness and stability, governance reach, unrest.
  - "Knowledge of the world": the implementation rule.
  - M3 acceptance: 10–60 settled civilizations by year 1,000 in every seed, and the border ratio ≥ 1.5.
  - Story health.
- User guidance: no deadlines or scripted outcomes; consolidation must emerge from graded incentives (memory). The 10–60 range is a health check on what the mechanics produce, never a quota in the code.

**Plan (to refine):**
- **The choice:** at settling, the tribe's view (built by `perception.ts`) lists the civilizations it has met whose land it sees next to its own, with what it can see of them:
  - kinship: the same people (lineage) and similar culture values;
  - the people it sees in their regions;
  - how well fed those people are, and how stable those regions are.
  - The pure choice in `decisions/` weighs each against founding its own civilization, weighted by Tradition and Expansionism.
- **Acceptance:** the civilization accepts with a graded chance from its own governance reach, measured as travel-km from its capital to the tribe's land.
- **Joining:** the tribe's groups pass to the civilization (`transferGroup`), each founding a village, and the tribe ends. The event is "band joined" with causes.
- **Stability system** (system 5, yearly per civilization, staggered): regional stability, an unrest flag and event, and an output multiplier in production.
- **Tests:** joining scoring units; integration (joined tribes, events, accounting); stability and unrest; acceptance in the M2/M3 runs; browser checks of the inspector's stability.
- **Study:** civilizations per century, joins, unrest, and acceptance at 1,000 for all five seeds.
