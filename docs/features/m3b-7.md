# M3b.7 — Cultivated land

**Feature / milestone / status:** VISION.md milestone M3b, slice 7. Status: verified (6 October 2026) and committed on `main`, not pushed, so CI has not run; the M3b close-out follows.

**Observable outcome:**
- **Each region keeps a set of cultivated cells:** its best farmland nearest its main settlement site, sized by the people who farm it.
- **Fields are cleared as farmers need them,** within about a year. Land farmed before it is cleared yields less.
- **Fields others have left fall fallow over the years.** After a famine, a people's fields shrink; they regrow as the people recover.
- **The map shows the cultivated land as an overlay** (the Fields layer). The region inspector gives its share of the region's farmland.

**Relevant spec sections:**
- VISION.md "Settlements and infrastructure", Cultivated land: "Each region keeps a set of cultivated cells, chosen from its best farmland nearest its settlements and sized by its farming population. It grows with farming and shrinks after famine, devastation or collapse."
- VISION.md "Region" data model: cultivated cells.
- VISION.md "Display data": cultivation display data in the separate simulation contract.
- M3b lab: "the cultivated-land overlay".
- M3b acceptance: "In each seed, at least one civ's cultivated land shrinks within 10 years after a recorded famine and later regrows."
- Devastation comes with war (M6).

## Acceptance (this slice)

- **The VISION.md line above**, measured in the five-seed study: the regions of a civilization's famine have less cultivated land within 10 years, and later as much as before or more.
- **Fields stay within the region's farmland** (an invariant). They grow toward what its farmers work and fall back when they are fewer.
- **The overlay draws exactly the cultivated cells** the simulation reports (browser check).
- **Gates:** M1–M3 and M3b.1–6 gates hold, and the complete `npm run check` passes.

## Decisions and defaults (rule 6)

- **Fields** (`fields.ts`; `FIELD_TUNING`): a region's fields are farm labour (people-equivalents) under cultivation, at most its farmland's (the food model's farm labour).
  - **Target:** each month the target is the region's farm workers that month (fieldFactor 1), at most its farmland.
  - **Clearing:** fields below the target are cleared at the target's whole a year (`clearRate` 1), so about a year from nothing.
  - **Fallow:** fields above it fall fallow by a quarter of the gap a year (`fallowRate` 0.25). Regions nobody lives in only fall fallow, and a remnant under one person's work is gone.
  - **Output:** while fields are below the target, farm output is × (0.6 + 0.4 × fields ÷ target): land farmed before it is cleared yields 60%.
  - **First version:** at 1.5 fields per farmer, regions at capacity were fully cultivated, so no famine shrank them (0 of 2,826 watched in Chronicle by 1,500). Fertile land worked beyond its farmland still stays fully cultivated through a small famine.
- **Ranking:**
  - each region's farmland cells (farm labour above 0) are ranked once by labour × 1 ÷ (1 + cells away from the region's best settlement site ÷ 4);
  - the fields cover the cells whose cumulative labour they reach;
  - settlements founded later do not change it.
- **The acceptance measure** (`watchFamine`, `checkFamineWatches`): each famine of a civilization records the fields of the regions it struck.
  - **Shrank:** within 10 years they fall below 98% of that.
  - **Regrew:** later they are back to it.
  - Watches end at regrowth, at 10 years without shrinking, or at 100 years. Measured on the struck regions rather than the whole civilization, which grows and unites meanwhile.
- **Contract** (protocol 17, rules 14):
  - the region map's `fieldRank` (u16 per cell; 65535 for no farmland), validated to be below its region's cell count and absent on water;
  - frames' `fields` (region, cultivated cell count), validated;
  - the inspector's `fields` (share of farmland labour, cells).
  - The region map is about 2.8 MB for a large world; the lab's limit rises from 4 to 6 MB.
- **Lab:**
  - the Fields layer (on by default) paints cultivated cells wheat-coloured, every other diagonal darker, over the territories and under the borders and marks;
  - it is recomputed only when the counts change;
  - the canvas reports `data-field-cells`; the inspector shows "Fields: 45% of its farmland cultivated (32 cells)".

- **M3b.6 review fixes:** recorded in that brief. They change the history; the results below are after them.

- **Most famines do not shrink the fields.** In dense farming regions more people work the land than its farmland's labour, so it stays fully cultivated through a famine that kills a few percent. Fields shrink where farmers are fewer than the land could take.
- **By 2,000, 92–98% of the world's farmland cells are cultivated:** the later centuries farm nearly all of it, and the Fields layer covers most civilized land.
- **Gates hold:**
  - M1: no silent bands; at most 9 months above 1.1× capacity;
  - M2: growth 4.2–5.6×;
  - M3: 22–27 civilizations at 1,000; border ratio against civilization land 1.05–1.17;
  - M3b.1: 87–93% of settlements by water;
  - M3b.4: 3–8 wonders by 2,500;
  - M3b.5:
    - road coverage 83–100% at 1,500 (Verdant: 5 of 6);
    - every seed reaches Engineering (1,709–2,960), with its first bridge 2–10 years later;
  - M3b.6: no century of the first 1,000 years in which world population fell.
- **Famines:** 2–3 a year worldwide in the later centuries, after the store fix. The strongest cause is drought or a bad harvest in about equal parts.
- **Events:** 64,000–96,000 by 3,000. Tier changes are 23,000–41,000 (M3b.5: 14,000–25,000), with more towns near the thresholds.
- **Speed: 65–98 ms per simulated year with invariants.** The performance pass comes before the M3b tag.
